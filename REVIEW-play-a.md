# Post-refactor review: play-a (combat core, giants and held rolls)

Scope: the combat core of `backend/internal/play` (lifecycle and turns, rollAttack/castSpell/rollDamage/TakeAction and what they call, movement and opportunity attacks, death, undo and concentration, the held rolls, highlights/summary/vitals/xp) plus its tests. Reviewed on `main` at 33927224 (train 12). Train 13 (#455) had not merged when this was written; it carries the last splits of the other session's area (contests window sync, hide reveal, puzzle rules), none in this area's files, and the parts of its content that touch play-a files were not seen. Read-only: nothing was run beyond `go build`/`go vet`/the complexity tools; no database, no tests.

## Verdict in five lines
- Behaviour equivalence: for every split on a write or player-facing read path in this area (EndTurn, StartEncounter and setup, ListCombatLog, the combat view, SpendMovement/OfferOpportunity, MoveCombatant, RollDeathSave/ConfirmDeath, the undo chain and every undoer, ResolveConcentrationSave/afterDamage, rollAttack, castSpell, rollDamage, TakeAction, ApplyPendingDamage, AdjustCombatantHitPoints, the roll-mode, rider, resource and held-answer RPCs) the old and new bodies were compared: same order of checks, same `db.InTx` boundaries, same writes, events, publishes and idempotency keys. No RN-10 or RN-18 regression introduced by a split was found.
- One undeclared behaviour change inside a "behaviour frozen" commit: the order of a cast's settled damage is now deterministic (play-a-4).
- Old gaps the review surfaced, none made worse by the splits: the highlights read still shows a reserved character (play-a-1), the drag refusal reveals an unseen creature (play-a-2), the rage-end log hint ignores a hidden NPC (play-a-5), the undo of a drag is half done (play-a-6), RN-18 on a weapon hold's answer is implicit and late (play-a-3).
- The main maintainability cost is duplicated plumbing from sessions that did not coordinate (the caller/key/encounter prologue, `touch`, `listCombatants`, five "pointer or zero" helpers) and `flow` structs with 14 to 35 fields whose phase order is a hidden contract (rollAttack, castSpell, GetTurnOptions, MoveCombatant).
- The test suite is mostly worth keeping; about 2,800 lines (2,300 of them golden) and about 110 to 140 full-fight DB scenarios can go without losing a guard (table at the end).

### play-a-2 | behaviour | high | backend/internal/play/combat_drag.go:68
What is wrong: RN-10 hole in the real drag (verified in the code). `dragTo` checks the landing square with `dragRoom(cs, grappler, dragged, sq, nil)`: with a nil viewer ANY other combatant counts, hidden ones too, and the refusal `NO_ROOM_TO_DRAG` ("there is no room to leave the creature") goes to the player. Proposed fix: make `dragRoom` return the blocking combatant instead of a bool. In `dragAlong` (`combat_move.go:776`), when the blocker is unseen by a non-master viewer, return the same generic text the walk uses (`MOVE_BLOCKED`, "an enemy is in the way"), or cut the drag like a walk (`StoppedEarly`) if product agrees.
Changes behaviour: yes (the error for the unseen-occupant case).
Guarded by test: none for the hidden case (TestADragWithNoRoomBehindIsRefused and TestMoveCombatantFrozen_ADrag use a visible occupant).
