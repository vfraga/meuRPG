# Combat screens

The combat screens live in `pages/live-session/combat/` and `shared/combat-map`, `shared/combatant-token`.
The browser draws what the server sends (reach from `GetMoveOptions`, totals, verdicts); it computes no rule.

## Tokens

- **Two kinds, told apart by shape, never by colour:** a player character is an `ink` disc; an NPC is a rounded square with an `ink` border (28% corner); a character's creature is a round, hollow disc with a dashed `ink` ring.
- **The initial** (or two letters and the number of a copy, "G2") is in Alegreya 700. Sizes: 22 to 34 px on a map, 28 in lists, 48 in the bar.
- **The turn** is a 3 px `accent` ring outside. The player's own token has a 2 px `ink` halo and never the turn ring alone: a wolf's turn is the ring and the word "Vez", 11 px clear of the token so it never touches its face. The dashed ring is never `accent`.
- **Defeated:** grey with ✕. **Hidden (master only):** dashed with the `visibility_off` seal, shown only when the map is at least 480 px wide.
- On a map the token uses the fixed `--mr-map-token-*` colours in both themes.
- **Small squares:** a token on the map is 86% of its square (at most 40 px) times the squares it covers, never more than its squares, and its border and the rings (the turn, "you", the choice of a target) shrink with it, so nothing drawn around a token reaches over its neighbour's square.

## The turn

- **Order and turn card:** the player's turn is a 2 px `accent` frame: the token, "Sua vez, Pensantus", "Rodada 3 · depois de você: Toren", the economy as shapes and one line of the character's own numbers ("PV 23 de 23 · CA 12 · 1º ○○○● 2º ○○": hit points, armor class and the slots; never another creature's numbers, RN-20). Off turn the card says whose turn it is and who comes next.
- **Outlined actions have an ink label** ("Atacar", "Conjurar", "Mover", "Encerrar turno"): the outline is `control-line` and the words are `ink`; the accent is for the one filled button and for links. The spell's "?" is 44 px and named ("Detalhes de Sono"). The hint of a tap on the map ("Adaga · toque num inimigo com anel.") is `ink` at 14 px. On a phone the map comes right under the header (a 12 px step, not 24), and under 360 px the bar of what is left drops its "Ainda tem" word to a screen reader, so the page keeps a line. A move that opens a door says so in a toast ("Você abriu a porta."), besides the log line and the map.
- **The economy is shapes** (`app-economy-shapes`, `app-economy-mark`, `shared/economy-shapes`): a circle for the Ação, a triangle for the Bônus, a square for the Reação, a walker for the metres left. A shape is filled while it is there; spent, it is a dashed outline with a slash and the word in `ink-muted`. The words ("Ação", "Bônus", "Reação", "9,0 m") stay beside the shapes and `aria-label` says the state ("Ação bônus usada"), so a shape is never the only sign. With Extra Attack the Ação stays filled while attacks remain ("Ação, 1 ataque restante"); a creature that cannot act has all three spent. The same marks tell each row of the tabs which economy it spends ("Ação", "Bônus", "Reação", "Sem custo" for what costs nothing, such as Surto de Ação).
- **Movement is a row under the map** (`app-move-row`, inside the map card): the walker, "Restam 9,0 m · 6 quadrados" with a bar, and on a phone the one "Mover" button (44 px, dashed with its reason while an answer is awaited, absent when there is nothing left to move or the character is grappled: "Indisponível: Agarrada."). Nothing floats over the map but the map's name and the "Ver mapa" icon. From 1024 px the row has no button ("arraste o seu token no mapa") and "Mover" is in the turn bar at the top. There is exactly one "Mover" button on every screen, and one "Encerrar turno".
- **The bar of what is left:** on a phone a bar above the pinned nav says "Ainda tem" with only the shapes that are still there ("○ Ação ■ Reação 🚶 9,0 m") and holds "Encerrar turno", which is an outline until the Ação and the Bônus are spent and asks "Ainda tem ação disponível. Encerrar mesmo?" otherwise. A tap while the turn's options are being re-read waits for them (`aria-busy`). The bar's height is kept in the page's `scroll-padding-bottom` (`keepClear`), so a focused field is never under it.
- **Layout:** the DOM reads turn card, notes, actions, order strip, map, log, vitals and the bar (the keyboard order); under 1024 px the map comes first on the screen (`order`), then the card and the tabs; on a phone the map is a wide window of about 160 px (354 px wide, `turnWindow`) around the player's token and the creatures near it, so the turn card and the tabs start on the first screen ("Ver mapa" has the whole map; choosing a target grows it to about 340 px). From 1024 px the turn bar is the first row and sticks under the page's bars (the card's host is `display: contents`, so the page's column is its room; its height is kept in `scroll-padding-top`): the token, the title, the shapes with the walker, "Mover" and "Encerrar turno" on one row. Below it, from 1280 px, three columns: "Ordem" (words for everyone else's hit points, "Você · 23 de 23 PV" for the person's own, and the turn card's "PV 23 de 23" too: the server sends a player's character's numbers through its vitals, which the page passes down when the combatant carries none), the map with its movement row and "Registro do combate" as a fold, and the actions rail; from 1024 to 1279 px two columns. The master's combat has its own layout, below.
- **Action tabs** (`action-groups`, `action-row`, `core/combat/action-tabs.ts`): one tab list built from what `GetTurnOptions` sends, its panels mounted and `hidden` (the ARIA tabs pattern of `app-tab-list`; each tab's count is also in its accessible name, "Magias, 4 magias"). "Ataques" holds the weapon attacks, the cantrips that attack, "Agarrar", "Empurrar" and "Escapar"; "Magias" (only for a caster) holds every spell in the order the server sends, with the slots on one line from 1024 px ("Espaços: 1º ○○○● 2º ○○"); the class's tab holds the abilities the class grants ("Guerreiro", "Chi" for a Monk, "Habilidades" when they come from several classes or from none the table knows), Wild Shape and a beast's "Voltar à forma normal"; "Outras" holds "Levantar-se", the standard actions and the extra action an effect gives. A row is the name with its economy mark beside it, one line under it (a spell's circle, "Concentração" or "Truque" first, then what the spell does) and its button with an icon beside the word (a wand for "Conjurar", swords for "Atacar"); a spell the character has from a grant is its own row, after the source and its uses ("Dom de teste · À vontade", "· 1 uso"), apart from the class's row of the same spell, and its cast sheet has no slot step unless the grant allows one ("Sem espaço (usa a concessão)"); a spell row has no "?" (the description is the cast sheet's own "?"), and the cantrip that attacks keeps one. While the target is chosen on the map, the row's button gives way to the tag "Escolhendo o alvo" on one line. In a combat with no map the movement is a line of the actions card, not a card inside it. The turn options carry no class name, so the tab is named from the feature that grants the action (`classTabName`).
- **A table feature that asks something first** (`action-spend.ts`, `app-table-action-sheet`): the row's detail line says what a use costs and what is left ("Gasta 2 · restam 5", or "Gasta de 1 a 3 · restam 5" when the player chooses). "Usar" on an action that has a `cost_max` above its least cost, a heal or temporary hit points opens its own sheet (a bottom sheet on a phone, so the sticky bars never cover it; a dialog from a tablet up) with: "Quantos?", a stepper (two 44 px buttons around the number, named "Gastar um a menos" and "Gastar um a mais") from the least cost to the smaller of `cost_max` and the uses left, with "de 1 a 3"; "Para quem", radio cards with "Você" first and then the other player characters the caller sees that are alive and not hidden (shown only when the action heals or gives temporary hit points and there is someone besides the actor; the server checks the choice again and refuses a creature or a dead one); a live line of what the choice will cost and give ("Gasta 3 · cura 3d6", from `heal`, `heal_per_point`, `temp_hp`, `temp_hp_per_point`; only a preview, the server decides); and the roll of the heal's dice in the two ways of rolling, or a plain "Usar" when nothing is rolled. The number goes in `TakeActionRequest.spend` and the ally in `heal_target_combatant_id`; the answer shows the hit points healed, the temporary ones, the dice and the points spent. An action with a fixed cost and no result is used at once. When fewer uses are left than the cost, the row is off with the reason "Faltam usos" and its detail reads "Faltam usos: gasta 2, restam 1".
- **A spent group folds to one line:** the rows of an economy that is spent (its Ação, Ação bônus or Reação used, or the character cannot act) are one folded row, "Ação bônus · Usada" (`app-fold`, `appearance="row"`, `muted`), that opens to what the group held; what costs nothing never folds, and neither does a row the server still offers (the follow-up strikes of Rajada de Golpes, the next attack of Ataque Extra): only what is over folds. The standard actions all spend the Ação, so once it is spent they are one folded line, "Ação · Usada", and stay open while it is there. A row is a name with its economy mark, a detail and a 48 px outlined button whose `aria-label` names the action. A disabled option has a dashed button with `aria-disabled` (still reachable), `block` and the reason linked by `aria-describedby`. A reaction that waits for its trigger has no button.
- **The actions rail** (`app-session-rail`, from 1024 px): the tabs sit in a column that sticks under the turn bar and scrolls on its own. Off turn it says what the character still has ("Reação · Disponível", "Reação · Usada"); a reaction keeps opening its own dialog.
- **Turn alert** (`core/combat/turn-alert.ts`): when the turn passes to a combatant the player plays, the screen warns once: vibration where the browser has it, a two-note Web Audio chime (only after the first tap on the page), a 0.5 s shake of the turn panel (`.mr-nudge`) and, while the tab is hidden, the title "● Sua vez · MeuRPG". It never fires on page load, on a repeated stream update, or for the master's or an NPC's turn. Under reduced motion the shake is a still 2 px outline. The switch "Avisar na minha vez (som e vibração)" turns off sound and vibration only and is kept in memory.
- **Master's nudge:** "Chamar a atenção" (`app-nudge-button`) fires the alert on one player's screen and shows "O mestre está chamando você." (`app-calling-notice`), which leaves by itself after 10 s.
- **Joint turn:** combatants with the same initiative play one turn. The "Turno conjunto" box is a 1.5 px `control-line` frame (never a side stripe); each member shows "Ainda age" or "Encerrou" as icon and word; each player ends their own part ("Encerrar a minha parte", outlined, asks in place); there is no "end the group's turn". The combat bar keeps "Próximo turno" in its place, dashed and `aria-disabled`, with the reason "Cada um encerra a parte no painel Turno" under it, on the desktop bar and the phone's pinned bar, so "Encerrar combate" never moves into its spot.

### The master's combat

- **Combat bar** (`combat-bar`): under the app bar, sticky from 1024 px. It has the round and the name, the token with "Vez do Goblin 1", "Em seguida: …", the tags that wait ("Falta aplicar 5 de dano", "Esperando 2 respostas"; during a reaction, "Falta responder por Seraphin" while the player's answer is due and "Falta o teste da Armadura animada" while the master rolls the aggressor's save), "Encerrar combate" (outlined, asks in place) and "Próximo turno", the one filled button on the screen: the conversation's "Enviar" is outlined while a combat runs. While the initiative is set up the bar holds "Começar o combate" (dashed and `aria-disabled` until everyone has rolled, the reason linked by `aria-describedby`) and "Cancelar combate".
- **Three columns from 1280 px** (`combat-view`, `master-layout`): "Ordem" on the left (sticky, its own scroll, "Mais N abaixo" when rows are out of sight), the map in the middle (no tabs under it: the map card keeps that height) and the rail (`app-session-rail`) on the right with "Turno" and "Conversa". The rail scrolls inside itself while its tabs and the combat bar stay fixed. The columns stick at the combat bar's measured bottom edge (`--combat-top`, read from the bar by `keepFocusClear`), never at a constant. Between 1024 and 1279 px the rail is under the map and the order is in the column; under 1024 px the phone layout. A theatre-of-the-mind combat keeps the turn card, movement and cover in the middle column. **Width:** from 1280 px the page around the master's three columns is not held to the 1280 px the other pages have (`main.app-content:has(.tri--wide)` in `styles.scss`: up to 2400 px, a 24 px side gutter instead of 48 px); "Ordem" (288 px, or the 64 px strip) and the rail (368 px) keep their widths and the map's column takes the rest, so a 40-column map has squares of about 12 px at 1280, 18 px at 1492 and 23 px at 1728 (a map column of 528, 740 and 976 px). The map's column scrolls on its own when the map is taller than the room under the bar.
- **The map's card** (`combat-map-card`, C7-02): one header row with the map icon, "Mapa", "Cripta do Corvo · 24 × 16 · 1,5 m" and "Ver mapa" (with its icon: the browser's full screen, or the map's own screen where there is none); under it the two switches "Alcance do Goblin 1" and "Movimento forçado" in one row, their explanations read by a screen reader (`aria-describedby`), not drawn. The legend reads "Personagem · NPC · Escondido (só você vê) · Vez"; there is no line about dragging.
- **Ordem** (`order-list`): 56 px rows with the token, the name, hit points as a number and a bar, and one state word by priority ("Derrotado", "Caída", "Morrendo · 3 falhas", "Escondido", "Concentração"); everything else (the spell, cover, effects, the challenge rating) is in the opened row. A tap opens the row in place (several can be open; a row with a question stays open): "CA 13 · 14 de 24 PV", "Concentração: Fogo das Fadas" (with the hourglass) and effects, then "Dano/Cura", "Condições" and "Mais" as three equal buttons on one line, the icon over the label. "Mais" (cover, side, reveal, remove) starts with the challenge rating of a monster ("ND 1/4"). The header button folds the column to a strip of tokens with bars; the fold is in memory only.
- **"Mais do combate"** (C7-02, C7-03): in the rail, under the content of "Turno", one fold per panel, in this order: "Registro", "Efeitos", then "Névoa e luz", "Armadilhas", "Tesouro" and "Quebra-cabeças", each only when it applies (the page projects those panels with `appMapTab`; "Quebra-cabeças" shows only with an open puzzle). Each row opens in place and several can be open ("Efeitos" is compact there: no frame and no drawn heading, one line per effect with its targets, the end and "× Encerrar", then "Adicionar efeito"; the projected panels lose their card's frame, so every row indents the same); a count ("Armadilhas", 2) sits beside the chevron. The strip "Mapa · Cena · Espelhar" under the map is not built: it appears only once a second option exists.
- **Rail:** "Turno" holds what waits for the master (held questions as cards, the answers, the damage to apply), the NPC card (compact in the rail: the bar already says "Vez do Goblin 1", so the card is named "Ações do Goblin 1" for a screen reader and draws no heading; who it is, three small boxes and the chips, then "Aplicar 5 de dano" / "Não aplicar"), the deaths block and then "Mais do combate"; "Conversa" is the session's conversation, with its unread count while it is not the open tab.
- **Phone:** the pinned nav "Combate · Grupo · Conversa" ("Grupo" is the party's panel in the session, as outside a combat; the combat waits behind it); inside "Combate" the tabs "Turno · Ordem · Mapa" (no "Mapa" in a theatre combat), the same rows of "Mais do combate" in a "Mais" fold at the end of "Turno", and a fixed bar with the waiting tag and "Próximo turno" (the NPC card has no button of its own). The page keeps `scroll-padding` under the sticky bars so the focus never hides behind them.

### The initiative setup

The setup (status `SETUP`) is the bar, the "Ordem de iniciativa" list and, beside or under it, "Quem está surpreso?", "Tudo pronto" and the starting positions. Its parts answer to the width they are given, not to the screen's: from 1600 px the page puts the conversation column (380 to 420 px) beside the board, so the board is about 740 px at every width above, while on a laptop it is up to 1200 px. Container queries do the work (`.setup-wrap`, the list, the bar), never the viewport.

- **Columns:** two columns (the list, and the 400 px side) only when the board has 1100 px or more; under that the side goes under the list. At 1280 and 1440 px the list is 760 px; from 1600 px the board is one column of about 740 px.
- **A row of the list:** from 760 px of list, one line: place, token, name and details, the roll ("1d20 (8) + 2 = 10", 218 px) and the buttons (190 px). From 600 px the name and details take the first line and the roll and the buttons share the line under it (the buttons wrap if they do not fit). Under 600 px, as on a phone: the words, then the roll, then the buttons, each on its own line.
- **A name** keeps one line and ends in an ellipsis; the full name is the tooltip (`title`). It is never broken letter by letter, and nothing in a row leaves it: the roll and the buttons are inside the row's box at every width from 1024 to 1920 px.
- **The bar's help text** ("Os NPCs rolaram sozinhos…") has a measure of at least 24 characters. From 1024 px, when the bar is narrower than 1100 px, the text takes the line under the title and the buttons, with the whole width; the dashed "Começar o combate" and "Cancelar combate" stay on the first line.

### The master acts for a player's character

On a player character's turn the master's "Turno" leads with that character's card (`act-as-card`, C8-01, C8-02): "Vez do Pensantus", "Mago 3 · o jogador joga na tela dele", three boxes (hit points, "CA", "Deslocamento"), the economy shapes, "Esperando Pensantus. Se o jogador não estiver, jogue este turno por ele." and one outlined 48 px button, "Agir por Pensantus". It is on every player turn (there is no "absent" mode); a downed character has the death-save card instead and no button. The page's `acting` signal holds the character the master acts for (`actFor`, in memory, cleared by the turn passing); while it is set, `own()` of the combat screen is that character, so the economy, the action tabs (`action-groups`), the sheets, target picking, the "Mover" page and the end of the turn are the player's own, with the master's rights (the server accepts the master on each path and writes "(pelo mestre)" in the log).

- **Rail:** the band (`acting-band`, accent border and soft accent surface) "Você está agindo por Pensantus" with "O que você fizer aqui é dele, como se ele fizesse." and an outlined "Voltar ao mestre" (it gives the rail back and keeps the turn), the shapes with the walker, "PV 23 de 23 · CA 12 · slots" and the character's tabs. The rail has no "Mover"; the master drags the token, or uses "Mover" in the map card's header, which reads "Toque no alvo · por Pensantus" while a target is being picked. The order row's tools stay.
- **Combat bar:** a tag "Você age por Pensantus" beside "Em seguida", and the one filled button reads "Encerrar o turno de Pensantus" (on a phone the pinned bar's button, and the target bar of C7-04 takes its place while a target is picked, with "Por Pensantus"; the phone's line under the title reads "Rodada 3 · você age por Pensantus"). A player's turn that the master has not taken keeps "Próximo turno" and the tag "Esperando Pensantus" in the pinned bar.
- **Targets:** the rows are the server's list for the character (its vision, reach and cover); a creature it does not see (`not_seen`) is dimmed, and a tap on it says "Pensantus não vê este alvo".
- **Death save first (C8-03, SRD 5.1):** a downed character's card (`death-save-due`, danger surface) leads "Turno" with "Toren está caído: teste contra a morte", the successes and failures as marks and words, one line of the rule, "Rolar por Toren" (filled) and "Digitar o resultado" (outlined) at 48 px, as the dice mode allows, and "O turno de Toren só passa depois do teste." "Próximo turno" is dashed (`aria-disabled`, still focusable) and the bar says "Falta o teste contra a morte"; the server refuses the end of that turn too.
- **A reaction nobody answered (C8-04):** after 10 s the player's optional reaction comes to the master at the top of "Turno" (`app-reaction-queue` with `part="late"`, above the held questions and the NPC card), on the warning surface: "Pensantus pode usar Escudo Arcano", the tag "10 s sem resposta", the numbers (the attack against the armor class, with and without the spell), the cost (the reaction and the lowest free slot, "1º nível (3 de 4 livres)"), and "Usar" / "Não usar", outlined, the same size. The bar says "Falta responder por Pensantus" and the tab count includes it. Nothing passes it by itself: the master answers, the player may too, the first answer wins and the other reads "Já respondido".
- **Rolling for someone (C8-05):** in the initiative list of the setup, "Rolar por Brisa" (the app's dice, with the character's bonus) sits beside "Digitar pelo jogador" for each player who has not rolled; in the group check and the scene's requests the same button, with the typed result when the table plays with physical dice. "Começar o combate" is dashed until everyone has rolled.

## Distance and units

Every distance the screen writes comes from `core/units.ts`: 1 square = 1.5 m = 5 ft.
Displacement is "7,5 m · 5 quadrados"; a reach or the distance to a target is in meters (`metersText`).
The number and unit travel together (non-breaking space) and a "·" never starts a line.

## Rolling and results

- **Attack sheet:** a bottom sheet under 768 px, a 480 px dialog above (`openSheet`). Three steps (Alvo, Rolar, Dano); a target out of reach is dashed with `block` and the reason.
  `app-roll-picker` offers the two RN-18 ways; the typing field's border is its focus (2 px `accent`, 2 px `danger-ink` on error); "Confirmar 16" is dashed until valid.
- **Target on the map** (C7-04, C7-05; `combat/target-mode/`, `PickLayer` of `shared/combat-map`): the player's "Atacar com X", "Conjurar X", "Agarrar" and "Empurrar" on a combat with a map bring the map into view (a scroll of `behavior: 'auto'` under reduced motion) instead of opening the sheet. The map's card takes the accent border (2 px), "Toque no alvo" beside its name from 768 px and, on a phone, shows the part of the grid that holds the character and the creatures offered, about as tall as it is wide. The creatures the server lists for the action (the same `TargetInReach` the sheet's list reads: reach, cover, the master's mark) are buttons of at least 44 px named "Escolher Goblin 1 como alvo", with a ring (a pale edge and the map's garnet, thicker once chosen); one that is out of reach or behind total cover is dimmed, its button says "Goblin 3: fora de alcance", and a tap on it says why in a chip over the map (from 600 px of map; on a narrower one in the bar's hint line, so the chip never covers a token) and in a polite status. A valid target past the weapon's normal range and within its long one has the tag "Longe: desvantagem" and is not dimmed (only "Longe" on a map narrower than 600 px or with several of them; the button's name has the whole). The chosen target wears "Alvo" with a check; a missile's dart is "×2" on the token that took two. The tags of a creature sit beside its token, on the right (the left at the right edge, below or above when neither has room), "Alvo" first and nearest the token, then "Longe"; the chip that says why a creature cannot be chosen goes in free map. `planPick` (`shared/combat-map/pick-places.ts`) lays them out in px (measured with a `ResizeObserver`) so no label touches another creature's ring, another label or the map's outer edge; a chip with no free place is not drawn and the bar's hint line says the reason. A tap picks the creature whose square is under the pointer (`creatureAt` in `shared/combat-map/pick-hit.ts`; a creature that is Large or bigger counts on every square it covers), not the topmost button: the buttons are 44 px wide, so on small squares they reach over each other's; on an empty square the nearest creature within 22 px of its centre is meant, and a click from the keyboard picks the focused button's creature. The map has a 12 px gutter and does not clip while the choice is open (a creature on the map's edge keeps the whole map on a phone, so the gutter holds its ring), a "Vez" word at a side edge leans inward, and on a phone the map's bar ("Ver mapa") is a row above the map instead of floating over it. The page scrolls the map card to its top under the app bar (its height is the card's scroll margin), and the bar's hint keeps to two lines so the card fits above it. The chosen target wears "Alvo" with a check; a missile's dart is "×2" on the token that took two.
  The bar (`app-target-bar`) is a region "Escolha do alvo" and the focus comes to its title. On a phone it sits above the pinned nav in the place of the turn's bar (so "Encerrar turno" is not on the screen while it is open): the icon, "Escolha o alvo · Raio de Fogo" and "Toque num inimigo com anel.", the link "Escolher na lista" (and "Desfazer" for darts), then an equal pair "Cancelar" | "Confirmar", the second dashed until the target is chosen and then the screen's one filled button. Once a target is tapped the bar reads "Raio de Fogo em Goblin 1" and "+5 para acertar · 1d10 de fogo · toque em outro anel para trocar". Darts and rays read "Mísseis Mágicos · 2 de 3 dardos" with "Cada toque põe um dardo; um alvo pode levar vários.", and a spell with several targets toggles each tap. From 1024 px the same component is a card under the rail's action tabs ("Escolha o alvo no mapa" with "Cancelar" and "Escolher na lista", then "Confirmar" once a target is named) and the action's row says "Escolhendo o alvo" in place of its button. "Confirmar" opens the same sheet at the roll with the target named (`AttackSheetData.target`, `CastSheetData.preset`, the first target of the contest's list); "Escolher na lista" opens it at its list, which is also the keyboard and screen-reader path; "Cancelar" and Esc return the focus to the action's button. The choice lives in memory (`TargetMode`) and ends when the turn passes. Area spells keep their placement on the map, and the master's sheets, an opportunity attack, a creature's attack and a combat without a map open the sheet at once.
- **Master's card** (`npc-card`): the screen's filled button is "Próximo turno"; "Rolar ataque" is outlined. The result is a block with its head ("Cimitarra em Brisa"), the formula, the pill ("Acertou", "Crítico", "Errou") and "contra CA 18 do Toren"; "Aplicar N de dano" (2 px `accent` outline) with "Não aplicar", which asks on the row. Passing the turn with damage pending asks "Há dano sem aplicar. Passar o turno mesmo assim?". The roll buttons are off exactly when a roll would be refused.
- **Cast sheet:** levels are radio cards with `slot-dots`; a slot with none free is dashed with "Sem espaço livre"; the last slot warns in `warning-surface`. A spell that lets the caster pick a damage type adds the "Tipo de dano" row. A concentration spell cast over another says so above the button.
- **Area spells:** on a map with a grid the cast sheet has two steps, "Onde ela explode" / "Para onde?" then "Quem está na área". The outline moves in the browser (no call while moving); a release places it and the server's squares and effective origin replace it. The map is a `role="application"` with arrow keys, Enter, Escape and C.
- **Reactions and opportunity attacks** open an `alertdialog` by themselves, with no close button or Esc, and focus on the safe choice ("Deixar passar", "Não atacar"). The result shows only what the player may read: their own numbers, never an NPC's total or armor class.
- **Death saves:** the hero on their turn has a `danger-ink` frame, two rows of marks (success ✓, failure ✕, empty ring) and "Rolar teste contra a morte". Only the owner and the master see the marks (RN-24); other players read the state word. The master's confirmation of a death is a question in place with "Ainda não" focused. When a table feature's `death_save_failed` hook stands the character up (`DeathSave.hook`), the result reads "Falha, mas o traço X entrou em ação: você volta com N PV. Nenhuma falha entra na conta." and, because the card of the dying is gone by then, the same sentence is the action note of the turn. A hook that did not heal adds " O traço X entrou em ação: …" to the result. The combat log writes the same hook after the save ("X levantou Toren com 6 PV") and, on an applied damage, the temporary hit points a table action gave ("ganha 2 PV temporários") and the hook that kept the target on its feet (`CombatLogDamage.hook`), in the log's usual line style; only the numbers that arrive are printed.
- **Cover:** each target states its own cover with the swatch pictogram and its source ("Meia cobertura (do mapa)", "Três quartos (marcada pelo mestre)"), never an object's name or an AC; total cover from the map removes the target from the list, a total cover marked by the master shows dashed with the reason.
- **Conditions:** the 15 SRD conditions in 44 px boxes in a dialog; they become tags under the name and an `accent` dot on the token.

## The map

`app-combat-map`: the image with a 1 px SVG grid, a token per square, "Vez" above whoever plays; reach is a 30% `accent` tint on the squares the server says are reachable inside a dashed circle the size of the remaining movement.
The chosen square has a 3 px frame with a check (refused: dashed, `danger-ink`, `block`). The master drags any token; a player drags their own on desktop on their turn and the "Mover" page opens on that square (dragging never moves by itself). The map is one focus point: arrows move the choice, Enter confirms; a hidden list gives each position as words.

The "Mover", "Saltar" and "Ver mapa" page (`move-page`) draws the server's reach, the cost beside the chosen square (always one decimal) and the legend. A move that can provoke shows a `warning` notice and "Desengajar (gasta a ação)"; a move into a known trap asks in place.
Fog, layers and doors are in [maps.md](maps.md).

### Spell zones

The areas Spirit Guardians, Moonbeam, Cloudkill, Web, Grease and Entangle keep on the map (RN-37) are a layer of `app-combat-map-card` (`app-zone-layer`), drawn from the squares the server sent, under the tokens and over the fog drawing; it takes no pointer and is `aria-hidden`, because the list is its accessible twin.
Each spell has a line pattern (vertical lines, dots, slanted lines, waves, triangles, horizontal lines) in one of the map's two inks, a 2.5 px outline with a white halo and a pill label "Raio Lunar · Bartolo" over the top edge (under the bottom edge at the top of the map), with a concentration icon. A zone of a hidden caster has a dashed outline and a crossed-eye icon on its label; only the master receives it.
The legend names each spell that has a zone, with the pattern's swatch ("Nuvem Mortal só você vê" when every zone of the spell is the master's alone).

"Zonas ativas (N)" (`app-zones-panel`) sits in the side column under the answers and the turn card, a `section` named by its heading, one `li` card per zone: the pattern swatch, "Raio Lunar · Bartolo", the area in meters, the save and damage ("Constituição CD 14 · 2d10 radiante, metade se passar", "4d4 cortante, sem teste"), "anda 3 m por turno", the notes, and chips "restam 9 rodadas" (from ten rounds, minutes; no chip with no count), "Concentração de Bartolo", "Aqui: Tor, Jabuti". A zone with hidden details (another caster's zone, for a player) shows the name, the area and the caster only. An empty list draws no card.
The master's buttons are 44 px: "Mover" (a zone that moves by an action) and "Encerrar zona", which asks in place ("Encerrar a zona?", "Não" holding the focus, "Sim, encerrar") and says a refusal under the question. A polite live region says "Zona criada: …" and "Zona encerrada: …" when the list changes, never on the first read.

"Mover" opens "Mover o Raio Lunar" (`app-zone-move-sheet`): a bottom sheet on a phone, a dialog from a tablet up. The map is the pick-a-square map (a click or the arrows from the zone's origin, Enter confirms), the zone's current footprint stays dimmed ("Ponto atual") and the footprint around the picked square is drawn as the preview, open floor: a wall does not cut it here, the server cuts it on commit. A player's reach is the dashed ring of 18 m ("Dentro do alcance, até 18 m." / "Longe demais: a área anda até 18 m do ponto atual. Faltam …"); the master has no limit and spends no action ("Para onde? Sem limite de alcance e sem gastar a ação de Bartolo."). Under the map "3 m · 2 quadrados" and who is caught on the new area. "Mover a zona" is the one filled button (48 px, off until another square in range is picked) and "Cancelar" the outlined one; a refusal reads in place and the sheet closes on success.

The save a zone asks of a player is the effect-save sheet: the title "Raio Lunar de Bartolo", "Magia que ficou no mapa. Você entrou na zona." (or "A zona atingiu você no começo do seu turno."), "Teste de resistência de Constituição" with ", CD 14" only when the server sent the DC (the target player's prompt has none, and no placeholder stands in for it), "Se falhar: sofre dano radiante. Se passar: metade do dano.", the bonus and the spell. A zone that restrains adds "Soltar-se gasta uma ação." The damage of a zone with no save reads "Zona de magia: Nuvem de Adagas" on the card.

## Gridless combat ("teatro da mente")

What depends on place does not exist without a map: no button is disabled "for lack of a map", and the mode's reason is written once ("Sem mapa, o app não sabe onde ninguém está.").
The master has a "Teatro da mente" tag, "Gastar movimento", the offer of an opportunity attack and a per-target cover editor; target lists drop distances and say "O mestre decide quem está ao alcance." No coordinate appears on any screen.

## Monsters, creatures and encounters

- **Bestiary and "Criar NPC"** (`pages/bestiary`) are master-only; others read "Só o mestre usa o bestiário da campanha."
- **Monsters in combat** are NPCs: the order row shows "ND 1/8" before the AC. XP at the end of combat lists each defeated creature ("Bandido 1 a 3 · ND 1/8 · 25 XP cada").
- **A creature's Multiattack** (`npc-card/multiattack.ts`, inside the master's attack card): the card reads the routines of the creature's stat block (`CreatureAction.multiattack`, through `CreaturesClient.statBlock`, kept in memory) and shows "Ataques múltiplos: 2 × Pancada" above the attacks; with several routines it is a select, "Ataques múltiplos", like the target's. The count is the server's (`economy.attacks_left`): while attacks remain the "Ação" chip reads "1 de 2" (dashed only when spent), the roll form stays and the line "1 de 2 ataques feitos" and the outlined "Encerrar os ataques" appear; when the last attack is made, or the master ends the routine, the chip reads "Usada" and the roll form gives way to "Ataques múltiplos concluídos." / "Ataques encerrados.". Ending is the page's alone, not kept on the server (the server's count of attacks left is the rule): a new turn, "Desfazer última ação" or a reload brings the form back, and offering the rest again is harmless. A creature without Multiattack, or whose stat block cannot be read, shows the card as before.
- **Encounter builder** (`pages/encounters`): the budget, total, band and maximum CR come from the server (`EvaluateEncounter`); the screen draws them. `app-count-stepper` and `app-hidden-switch` are its controls.
- **Creatures and Wild Shape** (`shared/wild-shape`, `shared/summon-sheet`): the server sends numbers and lists; a druid in a beast form has a "Na forma de Lobo" tag in the master's order; a concentration tag is one solid tag with the hourglass.
- **Familiar's eyes** (`shared/familiar-eyes`): the button appears for any familiar and the server decides whether it is in range; the question states the cost (blind and deaf; in combat it spends the action).

### A creature's other actions

The master's NPC card (`npc-card`) has, under the attack card, a section "Outras ações" (`app-creature-actions`, `npc-card/creature-actions.*`; the pure parts are in `core/combat/creature-action.ts`). It lists the actions of the creature's stat block that are no attack roll: the ones that ask a saving throw (`CreatureActionKind.SAVE`) and the ones that roll nothing (`OTHER`), read with `CreaturesClient.statBlock`, kept in memory. The attacks and the Multiattack are not in it. A player's screen has no such section: the stat block is the master's (RN-20, RN-38).

- **An action's block.** The name in bold and its usage when it has one, translated by `usageText` ("Recharge 5-6" shows "Recarga 5–6", "3/day" "3/dia"); then the lines the app read, in Portuguese: "Teste de resistência de Destreza, CD 13 · cone de 4,5 m" (the area is cone, line, sphere, cube, cylinder or "raio de 3 m ao redor dele"), "Dano: 2d6 de fogo + 1d4 de ácido, metade se passar" (or "nada se passar") and "Se falhar: Amedrontado por 1 minuto; repete o teste no fim de cada turno dele". An action with no roll reads "Sem teste: gasta a ação e lembra o texto." A note says how far the app reads it: "O texto diz algo que o app não calcula: leia o lembrete." (partial) or "O app não calcula esta ação: o texto é o lembrete." (text-only), and for those, and for every action with no roll, the SRD text follows under the tag "Texto do SRD, em inglês" (`lang="en"`). The outlined button "Usar" (bolt icon, label "Usar <action>") is 44 px high like the other buttons of the card.
- **Why "Usar" is off.** The button is dashed (`mr-button--off`, `disabledInteractive`) and a line under it says why: "A ação deste turno já foi usada." when the NPC's action is spent, "Escolha ao menos um alvo." when the action names targets and none is chosen. It is off, too, while a call is running.
- **The targets.** When some action needs targets (it asks a save and has no shape on the map, or the combat has no map) the list "Quem a ação atinge" (in theatre of the mind: "Quem a ação atinge (você decide quem está ao alcance)") appears above the actions, one check box per combatant that is not the NPC and not down. The hostile ones start checked; once the master picks by hand his choice stays for the turn, minus who leaves the list.
- **The area.** On a map, "Usar" on an action with a shape opens the dialog "<NPC> usa <action>" (`app-master-creature-area`, the same map as the spell's area picker, `app-area-map`): one step, "Para onde?" for a direction (a cone, line and cube take one of eight) or "Onde ela explode" for a point (a sphere and a cylinder), under the subtitle with the area's shape and size; a radius around the creature has no dialog and is used at once). The dialog lists nobody; the server takes who is inside, and the result says who. "Usar <action>" is off until the area is placed, "Cancelar" leaves nothing spent.
- **The result, inline.** Under the list a status region (`role="status"`, `data-testid="creature-action-result"`) keeps the last use: the action's name; for an action with no roll, "Lembrete registrado no registro do combate. A ação foi gasta."; for each target its name, the pill "Falhou" or "Passou" ("Passou: metade do dano" when the action halves, "Falha automática" when the target could not save) with a check or a close icon (never colour alone), the roll ("Teste de resistência: 1d20 (12) + 2 = 14 contra CD 13") and "Ficou Amedrontado" when it got the condition; then the notes ("Amedrontado por 1 minuto.", "O alvo repete o teste no fim de cada turno dele; o app não conta o tempo."); and the pending damage the use opened, applied or discarded right there (`app-pending-damages`, flat). The card leaves those damages out of its own list while they show here. The result goes when the turn or the round changes and when "Desfazer última ação" takes the use back. A refused use shows the server's reason in a danger notice.

**The rider offer** (`hit-rider/hit-rider-card.ts`, the same card as the monk's offers, shown beside the damage cards). After an NPC attack with a rider has landed its damage the master reads a notice with the paw icon (`pets`), the attack's name in bold and "em <target>"; under it one line, "Pedir teste de resistência de <target> (CD 10, Constituição). Se falhar: Paralisado" ("Se falhar: dano extra" when the failure gives no condition) or "Agarrar (CD de fuga 12): <target> fica agarrado"; the ability comes from the attacker's stat block and the line reads without it until it arrives. Two buttons, "Aplicar" and "Dispensar" (labels "Aplicar <attack>" and "Dispensar <attack>"), are off while an answer is running. A player never gets the notice.

**The log lines** (`core/combat/combat-log-creature.ts`, `combat-log-actions.ts`). A used action is a cast line: " usou Sopro de fogo: Brisa falhou (CD 13), 14 de dano; Toren resistiu (CD 13), 7 de dano. Amedrontado: Brisa, por 1 minuto; repete o teste no fim de cada turno dele", and for an action with no roll " usou Invisibilidade: lembrete, a ação foi gasta"; the master's copy keeps the SRD text as a note. A rider answered is " usou Garras em Brisa: Brisa falhou no teste (1d20 (8) + 2 = 10 contra CD 10); ficou Paralisado por 1 minuto", " ... passou no teste", " usou Constrição em Brisa: Brisa ficou Agarrado (CD de fuga 12)" or, when set aside, " dispensou o efeito de Garras em Brisa". The DC and the escape DC are the master's alone; the dice of a save are written for the master and for the owner of the target character; a player's line has the names, the pass or fail and the damage.

## End of combat

`combat-summary` is a `success-ink` frame with rounds, combatants and defeated; the master also sees "O grupo agora". In an enemies-XP campaign "Voltar à sessão" is outlined and the filled button is the XP one until XP is given.

## Proposal (needs a board first): "Ver mapa" during a turn

Not built. "Ver mapa" (the browser's full screen, or the map's own page) has no turn panel, so the master cannot move or aim from it: to act on a turn they close it and go back to the columns.
What the full-screen map would need while a turn is open:

- **Who acts.** The creature on turn (or the player character the master acts for) named in a slim bar, with its token ringed, so the master never moves the wrong one.
- **Move.** Drag the token or tap a square, with the reach, the cost beside the square and "Restam 9,0 m · 6 quadrados" always in view; "Desengajar" and the trap question where a move provokes or enters a known trap, as on the "Mover" page.
- **Aim.** Choosing a target on the map as on the page ("Toque no alvo", the rings, "Alvo" and "×2" tags, the chip that says why a creature cannot be chosen), with the count a spell asks for (darts, rays, "escolha até 3") and a confirm that says what will be sent.
- **The turn's economy.** Action, bonus action, reaction and movement left as chips in the bar, and "Encerrar turno" within reach, never under the map's own controls.
- **Undo.** The last move or choice undone in one tap before it is confirmed; after it, the log's undo as in the columns.
- **Back to the columns.** An obvious "Voltar" that keeps the selection and the scroll, and Escape does the same.
- **Small and touch targets.** Squares are small on a 40-column map; buttons of 44 px with the square under the finger deciding which creature is meant (the rule above), pinch and pan that do not move a token, and a zoom that keeps the acting creature in view.

Open questions: does the bar replace the turn bar or sit beside it; do the sheets (attack, spell, resources) open over the full-screen map or send the master back to the columns; is a player's "Ver mapa" the same screen; what happens to a reaction window that opens while the map is full screen; and how much of "Ordem" the master needs while there.
