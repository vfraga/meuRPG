# Live session

The session page shows what the master chose to show and what a player may read.
Every piece draws what the server sends (RN-10, RN-20); nothing hidden is drawn grey, it is absent.

## Frames and the meaning of burgundy

A 1 to 2 px `accent` frame means "this is on the players' screens now" or "this is the session": the shown image, the open scene, the session notice, the puzzle notice.
It always comes with a sentence and an icon beside it.
Other panels use a `line` border. A "burgundy border" is never used for anything else, including in-place questions.

## The session bar and the phone nav

- **Session bar** (`pages/live-session/session-header`, `app-session-header`): one row at the top of every live screen (60 px on a computer, 56 px on a phone), for the master and the players alike.
  It holds, left to right: the back link to the campaign (the campaign's name beside the arrow from 768 px, the arrow alone on a phone; its name is "Voltar para a campanha <nome>"), the title "Sessão 3" (the page's `h1`), the "Ao vivo" pill with "desde 20:12" ("desde 30/09 às 20:05" when it started on another day; "desde" goes on a phone), and at the right "Dados" (the free dice roller, below).
  The master also has "Iniciar combate" while no combat is on, the "Tela da mesa" button (from 1024 px, below) and the "Sessão" menu.
  The master's "Iniciar combate" is an outlined button from 768 px and the menu's first item on a phone: the master's "Sessão" is a 44 px button with three dots, and under 360 px "Dados" is an icon for everyone and the live status leaves the bar.
  "Iniciar combate" opens the dialog of the same name (a full-screen dialog on a phone) with the session's current map; it waits while the map is read, and a map that could not be read puts a warning notice with "Tentar de novo" under the bar and starts nothing.
  The dialog's lead says what the old "Combate" strip said: "Escolha quem luta e o app pede a iniciativa de todos." (the theatre of the mind explains itself in the dialog).
- **"Tela da mesa" button** (the master only, from 1024 px, `(min-width: 1024px)` in `session-header/wide-bar.ts`): an outlined button with the TV icon, before the "Sessão" menu, that opens the panel of [the table screen](#the-table-screen-tela-da-mesa).
  Its words follow the TV: "Tela da mesa" while no TV tab is open, "Na TV: Cena" (or "Mapa", "Imagem", "Combate", "Espera") while one is, with a pin marker when the master fixed the item, and "TV sem conexão" with a warning icon and the warning look when the last TV tab lost its connection.
  While a TV tab is open the button takes the accent look.
  Under 1024 px the button leaves the bar and the "Sessão" menu has a line "Tela da mesa…" (the TV icon, and under it a second line: "Nenhuma tela aberta", "Na TV: Cena · espelhando", "Na TV: Mapa · fixo" or "Sem conexão desde 21:14") that opens the same panel.
- **"Sessão" menu** (a Material menu, 250 px, `aria-haspopup="menu"`; the button has its word and a chevron from 768 px and three dots on a phone): "Copiar link da sessão" (RN-07), which turns the button into "Link copiado" for two seconds and, if the clipboard refuses, shows the link in a selected read-only field under the bar; and "Encerrar sessão…".
- **Ending the session** asks in place under the bar, in a `role="group"` marked `data-confirm` (clicks are ignored for the first 500 ms) on the warning surface: "**Encerrar a sessão 3?** Ela não reabre depois.", "Cancelar" (focused, with a 2 px accent ring, in the neutral ink) and "Confirmar encerramento", the only one outlined in the danger ink; on a phone the two buttons are full width, equal and 48 px high. Cancelling returns focus to the "Sessão" button; a failure says "Não foi possível encerrar a sessão." under the bar.
- **Reconnecting:** the pill and "desde" stay; the neutral notice, a full-width row under the title (one line on a phone), says "Reconectando… Última atualização às 21:14. Não dá para rolar sem conexão." (without the time on a phone), and "Dados" is off on a dashed border (still focusable, `aria-describedby` points at the notice).
- **Pinned phone nav** (`app-session-tabs`): under 768 px, "Cena | Ficha | Conversa" is fixed to the bottom of every live screen (64 px plus the safe-area inset), outside the page, so it stays usable while the chat covers it.
  The page keeps `--mr-session-nav` of room under its content, the full-screen chat ends above the nav, and the player's sticky turn bar sits right above it (`bottom: var(--mr-session-nav)`); at 320 x 568 both bars leave the turn usable.
  "Cena" is the page, "Ficha" is the player's panel inside the session (a player without a living character finds the notice that says so; the master's second item is "Grupo", below), "Conversa" the chat; the current one is `aria-current`; the count on "Conversa" (a 20 px pill in `--mr-accent`) is in words in the name and stays live while "Ficha" is open; only the current item is filled, and new messages show as the badge alone. The nav's top line is drawn outside its box (exactly 64 px), so the player's turn bar sits flush above it.
  The open section is a 48 px pill (`--mr-accent-soft`, the ink at 12:1 on it); in the dark theme it has a 2 px `--mr-accent` ring too, since the soft maroon alone is 1.1:1 against the bar.
  While the nav is pinned the page's footer links are not drawn (`body.mr-live-phone`), so nothing sits under it.
  For the master it is "Cena | Grupo | Conversa" ("Combate | Grupo | Conversa" in a combat, where "Grupo" opens the party's panel and the combat waits behind it): "Cena" and "Grupo" choose a tab of the board, "Grupo" carries the amber count of casts waiting.
- **Sticky bars never cover what has the focus** (`keepClear`, `shared/scroll-clearance`): while a bar sticks to the top or the bottom of the screen (the player's turn bar at 1024 px and up, the phone's bar of what is left), the page's `scroll-padding` on that side is the bar's height, its offset and 16 px, followed with a `ResizeObserver`; a bar that flows with the page leaves it clear.

## The master's session outside combat

`pages/live-session/master-board` composes the master's page when no combat is on screen: the tabs "Cena", "Mapa" and "Grupo", with the conversation in a rail.
Nothing here is drawn for a player, and no panel is rebuilt for it: the existing panels are placed in the tabs.

- **Tabs** (`shared/tab-list`, "Seções da mesa"): the ARIA tabs pattern, one tab stop and arrow keys. The tab is the query parameter `?vista=` (`cena`, `mapa`, `grupo`), so a reload or a shared link comes back to it; nothing is stored on the device.
  The default is "Cena" while a scene is open and "Mapa" otherwise; "Abrir cena" switches to "Cena". Every panel stays mounted and is `hidden` when its tab is not the shown one, so a half-written draft, a map and a form keep their state.
  The count sits in the tab's name, as words for a screen reader: "Cena" carries an amber count of new rolls while another tab is shown, "Grupo" the casts waiting.
- **Above the tabs:** the held rolls ("Perguntas pendentes") and the notice about a map that could not be read stay above the tabs for as long as they apply.
- **Cena:** the open scene is one card with the accent frame, and its parts follow one another under a rule each, every one with an icon at the left of its title:
  the open-scene lines (`scene-who`: "Aberta desde 20:20 para" with the players in the scene as chips, a count ("6 de 8 jogadores") and overlapping initials past three, "Quem está na cena" as a text action; under it who is out ("Toren não está nesta cena.", "Fora da cena: Sálvia e Jabuti.") and, for a scene open to only some players, the switch "Os jogadores podem entrar" with its state word, or "Toren entrou na cena às 20:41." after a player joined by themselves; a scene open to everyone keeps "Aberta para os jogadores desde 21:10" and no switch); the title with "Trocar cena" and "Fechar cena" as text actions with icons; "Em cena · 2 de 4" with "+ Pôr em cena" as a text action of the header;
  "Imagens da cena · 3 de 8"; "Ações e rolagens"; "Pistas"; "Ganchos e anotações · Só você vê" as a fold that starts folded. "Rolagens da mesa" closes the column as a one-line fold, outside the card.
  - **Em cena** is one compact row per NPC: an eye at the left (a 44 px button named "Esconder Lia dos jogadores" / "Mostrar Lia aos jogadores"), the token, the name with "Os jogadores veem" or, hidden, "Escondida dos jogadores" in bold (the eye is muted and the "Dar a fala" button is dashed, off and still reachable, since a hidden NPC never speaks), a "Fala agora" / "Dar a fala" button of a fixed width (the speaker's has the 2 px accent border) and a menu with "Tirar de cena" and "Abrir a ficha do NPC".
  - **Imagens da cena** is the row of thumbnails with their names (a tap shows one; the one on show carries "À mostra" and the accent frame) and a dashed "Da galeria" tile at its end; under it "Deixar com os jogadores" with its state word on one line, and "Parar de mostrar" as the one button. An image of the whole map asks first, in place.
    With the scene open to only some players a tap on a thumbnail opens a menu in place ("Mostrar “O porão” para": "Só quem está na cena", the default and checked, "Toda a mesa", "Escolher quem vê…", each with the names small under it) and the thumbnail wears a "Mostrar ▾" tag on hover, on focus and with the menu open (always where there is no hover). Under the row a line says who sees the image on show ("Pensantus e Brisa veem", "Só Brisa vê", "6 de 8 jogadores veem", "Toda a mesa vê", "Os jogadores veem"), with "Mostrar a toda a mesa" (only when the table does not see it yet) and "Mudar quem vê" as text actions. Both choosers are `who-chooser`: the first row "Todos" (a tri-state box), one row per player with the character's name and "na cena" / "fora da cena", a live line ("Pensantus e Brisa veem a imagem.") and Cancelar / Pronto, the last dashed while nobody is chosen; a sheet on a phone, a dialog from a tablet up.
  - **Ações e rolagens** has one line per action ("Percepção · CD 14 · 1 tentativa") with each player's roll as a chip beside it ("Brisa 16 ✓", "Toren 7 ✗": the verdict in an icon and in words). A chip opens the roll under its row (the formula, the mode, "Dar mais uma tentativa"). A roll that arrives while the page is open is "nova" until its chip is opened: the header and the action say "1 rolagem nova". "Só você vê a CD" and "Teste em grupo" (named "Pedir teste em grupo"; its form opens under the header) sit in the header.
  - **Pistas** list each clue with who has it ("Ninguém tem esta pista ainda.", "Com Brisa") and "Revelar" / "Revelar a mais alguém".
  With no scene open the tab holds the scene picker, the full "Imagem para os jogadores" panel and "Pedir teste em grupo". Puzzles close the column.
  An action's eye sits at the left of its row; hidden, the row says "Escondida" with the eye-off icon at the end of its line, and the eye is off when the scene's point is not on the open map. The players' side of a scene open to some ("Nesta cena: você e Brisa", "Entrar na cena") is described under the player's scene.

On the map (`move-overlay`, in the map's overlay): a dashed ring on each token chosen, a dashed line and ring to the destination, and after a move that stopped early the solid line walked, an X where it was headed and the pill "7,5 m · parou antes" (the X and the pill do not scale with the zoom). "Pôr o grupo no mapa" is `put-party-dialog` (a dialog of 760 px, a sheet on a phone): "Quem vem · 4 de 5" (a box, the token and the class; one left out says "fora desta vez"), "Formação" (Em fila, Em bloco, Em arco, with one line under it), "Onde ficam: toque num quadrado do mapa" (the map's picture with its layers, the tokens as dots and the corner as a frame; the arrows move it a square), and "Cancelar" / "Pôr 4 no mapa".

  "Grupo" has one row per character: the token, the name with the class and a condition ("Barda 3 · Envenenada"), "23 de 24 PV" with a thin bar and a word when low, the slots ("1º ○○○● 3 de 4", "Sem espaços de magia"), "Ajustar" as a text action and a menu with "Chamar a atenção"; "Dar XP" is in the title.

## The table screen (Tela da mesa)

The table screen is the TV at the table: a tab the master opens and drags to a screen the whole table looks at, and the panel with which the master chooses what it shows ([MR-056](../product/stories.md#mr-056-show-the-table-on-a-screen), [RN-10](../product/rules.md#rn-10-map-without-spoilers)).

### The TV page

`pages/table-screen` is the route `/campaigns/:id/table-screen`, without the app's bar and footer.
It is made to be read from a sofa, so its type is large: from 1600 px every size goes up one step (the header, the notice line, the panels).

- **One screen, no scroll.** From 900 px the page is as tall as the window, and the header, the notices and the stage share it; nothing scrolls.
  A list draws only the rows that fit and puts "+N" under it for the rows left out (`tv-fit`, [Web app](../architecture/web.md#the-table-screen-page)); a row is never cut in half.
  Under 900 px the page is one column of natural height that scrolls like any other page.
- **Header** (`app-tv-header`): the icon of the stage, a small line above ("Mapa · Mirathel · Sessão 3"; for the waiting screen only the campaign's name), the title of the stage (the map's name, the image's name, the scene's name, "Combate · Cripta do Corvo", "Combate sem mapa", or "Sessão 3") and the chip of the connection at the right.
- **Chips:** "Ao vivo" (the live pill) while connected; "Reconectando…" (pending look) once the connection or a read has been bad for 3 s; "Sem conexão" (pending look, after a minute) and "Encerrada às 23:40" (neutral).
  A chip always has its icon and its words.
- **Late line:** under the header, a warning notice "Esta tela pode estar atrasada desde 21:14. Ela continua assim por até 1 minuto." while the chip says "Reconectando…".
  After a minute without a connection the stage is hidden: the card "Sem conexão desde 21:14" says that the screen hid what it showed and comes back by itself.
  The line has a polite live region of its own, always in the page, so its coming is read.
- **Notice line:** when the master's pin could not be shown, a plain line under the header says why ("O combate acabou. A TV foi para a tela de espera.", "A cena está aberta só para alguns jogadores, então a TV não a mostra.", "O mapa tem névoa e a Visão do grupo está desligada, então a TV não o mostra.").
  A change of stage is announced to a screen reader ("Na tela: Combate · Cripta do Corvo") in a polite status.
- **Full screen:** a button bar at the top, "Pressione F11 ou clique na tela para pôr em tela cheia.", shows where the browser can; it goes away when the tab is full screen or once used.

| Stage | Layout from 900 px | Under 900 px |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Waiting | Centred: "Nada à mostra ainda", "O que o mestre mostrar à mesa aparece aqui." and "Na mesa" with the characters (up to 12, the rest as "+N") | The same, centred |
| Map | The map in a card, with the free rolls beside it (a column of 260 to 480 px) | The map, then the rolls |
| Image | The picture as large as the room allows, with its name under it | The picture, then the name |
| Scene | The scene's text, and beside it "Em cena" with the NPCs the table sees (up to 8, the speaking one marked "Fala agora"); with no NPC the text takes the whole width | One column |

| Combat without a map | "Ordem" in the left half, and the rolls and the log in the right | One column |

- **The map** (`app-tv-map`) is the player's map component with its zoom, fit and full-screen buttons off, as tall as its card allows: the picture never pushes the legend out of the card.
  A map with the fog on is drawn from the group's tiles, black where the group does not see.
- **"Ordem"** has one row per combatant the table sees: the token, the name and one word (RN-20), "Vez" for whoever has the turn (a larger token), and "Vez do mestre" with an eye-off icon when the turn is one the table does not see; the round is in the title.
  When the list is longer than the room, the last rows are replaced by "+N".
- **The fog card** (`app-tv-fog-card`): a map icon, "O mapa fica na tela de cada um." and "A névoa está ligada e cada jogador vê só o que o próprio personagem vê.", in a panel in the place of the map, only in a combat whose map the fog hides (the order, the rolls and the log stay).
  A mirrored or pinned map the fog hides is not drawn at all: the waiting screen shows, with the notice line "O mapa tem névoa e a Visão do grupo está desligada, então a TV não o mostra."
- **The refusals and the end** are one card each, centred, with a large icon: "Só o mestre abre a Tela da mesa." (a lock), "Entre para abrir a Tela da mesa." (the page then sends the viewer to sign in and back), "Não deu para abrir a Tela da mesa." (it tries again in 10 s), "Carregando a mesa…", and "A sessão 3 terminou às 23:40." with "Até a próxima sessão de Mirathel." and "O mestre pode fechar esta aba." under the header chip "Encerrada às 23:40".
- **At 390 px** the TV page is the phone layout above: one column, every panel whole, the page scrolls, and the fit hides nothing.

### The master's panel

The panel is the sheet frame (`app-sheet-frame`, small title) opened by `openTableScreenSheet`: a bottom sheet on a phone, a dialog from a tablet up.
It fits one screen in its longest state, at 390 x 844 and at 1280 x 800: the frame's rows sit closer than in the other sheets, and no state makes the body scroll.

- **Title bar:** the TV icon, "Tela da mesa" and a tag at the right: "Aberta" (success look with a check), "Fechada" (neutral), "Abrindo…" after "Abrir a Tela da mesa" until a TV connects, and "Sem conexão" (pending look with a warning icon).
- **States of the body:**
  - Reading: "Lendo a Tela da mesa…".
    A failed first read is a danger notice "Não deu para ler a Tela da mesa." with "Tentar de novo".
  - No TV open: one line, "Abra a Tela da mesa na TV; depois escolha o que ela mostra.", and no rows.
  - Lost: a warning notice "Sem conexão desde 21:14. Depois de 1 minuto a TV esconde o que mostra; volta sozinha quando a conexão voltar."
  - A choice that did not go through: a danger notice "Não deu para mudar a Tela da mesa. A TV continua como estava." with "Tentar de novo"; it replaces the rows, so it is never out of sight.
  - A TV open: "Na TV agora" with the icon and the name ("O mapa **Cripta do Corvo**, fixa."), or the neutral notice of the pin that could not be shown in its place, then the rows.
- **Rows:** "O que a TV mostra" is a radio group.
  The first row is "Espelhar o que eu mostro" ("A TV segue o que você mostra à mesa"); under "Fixar" come "Só o mapa", "Imagem", "Cena", "Combate" and "Tela de espera", each with the name of what it would show under its title.
  Every row is a real radio button (the arrow keys move the choice; a screen reader hears the title, the line and "selecionado, 2 de 6"), at least 50 px high.
  The chosen row has a 2 px accent border, the soft accent surface and the filled radio dot, so the choice is never only a colour.
  While a choice is on its way no row can be picked.
- **Dashed rows:** a row the table cannot fill is dashed, with a "not allowed" mark where the radio dot would be, muted, and cannot be picked; the line under its title is the reason ("Nenhum combate agora.", "Nenhuma imagem à mostra.", "Nenhuma cena aberta.", "A cena está aberta só para alguns.", "Nenhum mapa agora.", "Precisa da Visão do grupo (editor do mapa).").
- **ⓘ:** at the right of "Espelhar", a 44 px button "Como o Espelhar escolhe" (`aria-expanded`).
  Open, it takes the place of the pins: "A TV mostra o primeiro que houver:" and the list "uma imagem mostrada a toda a mesa; o combate; a cena aberta a todos; o mapa; a tela de espera.", and "Na TV agora" steps aside so the panel does not grow.
- **Foot** (never scrolls away): the privacy line with an eye icon, "A TV mostra só o que toda a mesa pode ver. O que é só seu, ou só de alguns jogadores, fica fora."; then "Abrir a Tela da mesa" (a link that opens a new tab; filled in the phone's sheet, outlined in the dialog) and "Copiar o link" (outlined, 44 px).
  "Copiar o link" turns into "Link copiado" with a check for a moment; if the clipboard refuses, the address is shown in a selected read-only field "Endereço da Tela da mesa".
  While the tab opens, "Esperando a tela se conectar." is read under the buttons.
- The link is only the tab's address, with no query and no key; the panel stores nothing in the browser.

## Images shown to players

- **"Imagem para os jogadores"** (`pages/live-session/shown-image-panel`): with a scene open it is the scene's image row (`appearance="row"`: "Da galeria", "Parar de mostrar", the keep switch); otherwise the panel. Empty it says "Nenhuma imagem à mostra." with an outlined "Mostrar imagem"; showing, it has a 1.5 px `accent` frame, the thumbnail, the tag "Mostrando agora" and "Parar de mostrar" / "Trocar imagem".
  Stopping acts at once (showing again undoes it) and moves focus to "Mostrar imagem"; showing moves focus to "Parar de mostrar".
  Every change is announced in a `role="status"`.
- **"Deixar com os jogadores"** is a `role="switch"` that only asks: the server decides and the state comes from the response. The master's list "Deixadas com os jogadores" removes an image with "Tirar".
- **Players' blocks** (`shown-image-block`, `left-images-block`): "O mestre está mostrando" (an `accent` frame) and "Imagens que o mestre deixou (2)" (a `line` border, a fold). The image frame takes the image's proportion and the card's width, at most 40 % of the screen's height and 360 px, a taller image letterboxed on `--mr-ground`, so the scene under it is never pushed off the screen; it never takes focus; a `role="status"` announces changes; "Ver em tela cheia" follows the state (if the master removes the image, it closes). The images left are a grid of two columns of thumbnails, each one a button named "Ver <nome> em tela cheia"; on a phone the block is a fold of one row; from 768 px with an image shown it sits inside the image's card under a divider (the card ends after the thumbnails), and with no image shown it is a card of its own, open. See [The player's scene](#the-players-scene-and-the-ficha-panel).
- **Picker** (`shared/gallery-picker/image-picker-dialog`): a 720 px dialog (full screen on a phone). An image that shows a whole map carries "Mapa inteiro"; where the picker shows it to players, choosing it asks first ("Mostrar o mapa inteiro?", "Voltar" with focus, "Mostrar mesmo assim"). An image that is the background of a hidden map carries "Fundo de mapa escondido".
- **A player's image only while it is shown, left with the players, or from a map they see** (RN-10): the screen keeps nothing for later.

## RP scenes, stage, clues and notes

## Rolls

- **Dice animation** (`shared/roll-overlay`, mounted once in the shell) plays when this screen rolls in the app: attacks, damage, scene checks, saving throws, initiative, grapples, reactions, hit dice and the like.
  It never plays for a physical die the player typed, for another person's roll arriving by stream (the free dice roller's table roll is the one exception, below), or outside play (editors, campaign pages).
  A word ("Passou", "Falhou") appears only where the same screen tells it to this viewer; the DC and the other side's result are never shown.
  It is a native popover (`popover="manual"`) so it lies above open sheets; the picture is `aria-hidden` and one polite sentence is read when the dice land; focus does not move.
- **The tray** (`RollTray`) is one `<canvas>`: a felt with a wooden rim, with the dice thrown in, seen from above.
  The dice roll for about 1.0 to 1.8 s (never past 3 s), bump into each other and the rim, and stop on the faces the server rolled, with their numbers within ±10° of upright.
  Each screen picks its own seed, so the same roll looks different on two screens; the result never differs.
  The d20 and the tens die of a d100 are garnet, every other die is paper; numbers are ink, or light on garnet.
  A d4 keeps its numbers at the corners and its result upright at the top corner; a d100 is two d10, labelled "dezenas" and "unidades".
  When the dice rest, the numbers on the sides fade and only the result stays strong.
  A d20 that advantage or disadvantage left out is drawn at half strength and labelled "descartado"; a critical glows gold with sparks and "Crítico!", a fumble darkens, shakes and says "Falha crítica".
  Meaning is always in words as well as colour.
- **Size:** on a phone the tray spans the overlay's content width (16 px gutters) with a height of 0.55 times its width; from a tablet up it is at most 520 × 280 px.
  The dice get smaller as there are more of them. At most 8 dice are drawn; the rest is a "+N" chip.
  The label, the line, the note and the outcome stay under the tray and appear when the dice have landed.
- **Closing:** a tap or Esc while the dice roll lands them at once; a second tap or Esc closes; the overlay closes by itself after 10 s at most; a new roll replaces the one in flight.
- **Sound:** off by default, and quiet when on.
  The "Som dos dados" button in the overlay (`aria-pressed`, 44 px) switches it on or off; the sound is synthesized, with nothing to load, and the browser starts it from that tap.
  It lasts for the open tab: the setting lives in memory (`DiceSoundSetting`), never in web storage or a cookie, so a reload starts silent.
- **Reduced motion** (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, or a browser without `matchMedia`): nothing moves.
  The dice appear at rest, in rows, with their results, in a card that neither dims nor blocks the page, with "Som dos dados" and "Fechar" under the words; Esc and the 10 s close it too.
- **Colours** are the tray tokens ([tokens.md](tokens.md#dice-tray-tokens)); the canvas reads them from the page, and draws its numbers in the app's own Alegreya (never a font from a third party).
  The module (`shared/roll-overlay/tray`, plain TypeScript with no Angular) loads with the first roll, so the first screen does not carry it.
- **Natural 20 and natural 1:** a check, saving throw, contest, group check, scene roll or initiative whose counting d20 came up 20 or 1 carries a small `mr-tag` "20 natural" or "1 natural" next to its formula (`naturalMark`, `core/combat/natural-mark.ts`).
  It is information only: those rolls have no automatic success or failure on a natural die, so the tag never says "crítico" or changes a verdict. Attacks keep their "crítico" wording; an attack that misses on a natural 1 reads "Errou · 1 natural".
  A player sees it only on a roll whose face they already read.
- **What a use costs:** a choice that spends something says what, in words, beside the button, with what is left ("Gasta 1 ki · restam N"), never an armor class or an NPC's number (RN-20).

## Experience and level up

- **Who receives** (`xp-recipients`, `app-check-box`): one row per character with the name, a lower line and "+116 XP" or "Não recebe" in words (the empty box is never the only signal).
- **Split line** (`xp-split`): the big number and the written calculation; the polite live region only says "116 XP para cada.".
- **The two buttons** (`xp-actions`): two real buttons of the same size, 44 px side by side on desktop, 48 px stacked on a phone, the filled one first; while invalid the filled one is dashed with the reason above it, linked by `aria-describedby`.
- Dialogs ("Dar XP", "Registrar marco") use `sheet-frame` so the title and footer stay in reach at 320 × 568.

## Traps, treasure and rests

- **Trap card, treasure card, "Quem notaria"** (`app-trap-noticers`): the table shows the server's numbers (passive Perception, the dim-light deduction, where the character is, whether they notice); the browser neither sums nor compares.
- **Waiting damage:** an amber card in an `aria-live` region with a number field ("Mude o número se houver resistência: o app não calcula."), the HP before and after, "Aplicar N de dano" (the only filled button) and "Não aplicar", which asks in place.
- **Rests** (`rest-card`): the master's "Descanso curto" / "Descanso longo" read the server's preview and open an `alertdialog`-style question in place that lists what comes back. A resource that gives back one use (`RestResourceBack.back`) reads "Kai +1 uso" instead of "3 de 3". Each on_event hook of the owner for that rest is one more line, "Ao terminar o descanso longo, Kai: Fôlego da Mesa entrou em ação: ganha 3 PV temporários." (`hookSentence`, `core/combat/hook-text.ts`); a heal from dice is marked "(em média)" because the preview shows the average and the rest rolls the dice. The line prints only the numbers the server sent (the master and the owner), never a computed one.
- **Treasure** (`pages/treasure`): generated by the server; "Pôr no mapa" asks for a hidden point; gold becomes XP only in a gold campaign.

## Puzzles

The player's and master's screens draw only what the server sends: no solution, minimum or victory maths in the browser.
`PuzzleHost` is the one place that knows which screen belongs to which kind and returns the move the server accepts.
The master's live view labels what only he knows with "Só você vê". The player's notice uses the session's burgundy frame and a 48 px "Abrir o quebra-cabeça"; the puzzle page opens inside the session page.

## Generated images

The "Gerar imagem" dialog (`shared/image-generate`) asks the server for an image, shows the result in the gallery (hidden, RN-10) and lets the master show or adjust it.
Each field states in one sentence what goes to Google and says not to write names of people; the SynthID watermark is stated once in the footer.
A generated image carries "Gerada por IA"; an adjusted one "Editada de Imagem 1".

## Campaign document and gallery

- **Gallery** (`pages/gallery`): cards with the thumbnail, name, size and where the image is used; deleting an image that is a map's background is refused by the server and the card names the maps. The filled button is "Enviar imagem", so the delete confirmation is outlined in `danger-ink`. The quota becomes a `--warning` notice from 90%.
  The image window is a native `<dialog>` with the app's only large shadow; an upload error notice uses the server's `reason`, never its message.
- **Campaign document** (`pages/campaign-document`): Markdown read and drawn by `app-markdown` (our parser, no `innerHTML`); `[text](map:<id>)` and `[text](character:<id>)` open windows; a deleted target becomes text; only `https://` links open in another tab. The editor keeps the draft in memory only and asks before leaving ("Sair sem salvar?", never `beforeunload`).
