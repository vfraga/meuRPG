# Glossary

Using the same words in conversations, documents and code avoids confusion.
Each entry gives the Portuguese term as the app shows it, its English equivalent where one exists, and what it means in MeuRPG.
Entries are grouped by subject and sorted alphabetically (by the Portuguese term) inside each group.

Portuguese names of SRD terms follow the official Brazilian terminology (names and short terms only, never the books' text).
Where the product chose otherwise, the product wins: "Derrubado" for Prone, so it is not confused with "Caído", a character at 0 HP.
The general terms are fixed:

| Portuguese | English | Never |
| --- | --- | --- |
| habilidade | ability | "atributo" |
| nível da magia | spell level | "círculo" |
| teste de resistência | saving throw | "salvaguarda" |
| perícia | skill | |

Where a spell level could be confused with the character's level, the text says "nível da magia" and "nível do personagem".

## Campaign, people and access

| Português | English | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Campanha | Campaign | The ongoing story of one table: its sessions, characters, maps and documents. |
| Convite | Invite | A link that adds a player to a campaign. It can require approval ("Exigir aprovação do mestre"): whoever accepts becomes a pending member and creates a character, which the master approves, refuses or sends back (RN-15). |
| Handle | Handle | The nickname of a player without a Google account. It is unique within the master's table (RN-17). |
| Jogador | Player | Whoever plays in a campaign. Joins through an invite. |
| Link da sessão | Session link | A link that takes a member straight to the live session. It gives nothing to someone who is not a member. |
| Link do personagem | Character link | A link that hands a reserved character to the player who opens it, signs in and presses "Assumir este personagem". It works once, for 1, 7 or 30 days, and a new link revokes the previous one. Signing in with it never claims. |
| Membro | Member | A user in a campaign, as master or player. The role holds only for that campaign. |
| Membro pendente | Pending member | Someone who accepted an invite that needs approval. Sees only the campaign name and their own character, and becomes a player when the master approves (RN-15). |
| Mesa | Table | The set of campaigns of one master. |
| Mestre | Master (GM) | Whoever runs the campaign. Whoever creates a campaign is its master. A campaign can have more than one, and a master can hand it over (RN-13). |
| Nome de exibição | Display name | The name other members see. The person types it in the app. |
| Pedir ajustes | Ask for changes | The master's third answer to a pending character, besides approving and refusing: it goes back with a reason (1 to 500 characters), the player edits it and taps "Enviar de novo". Only the master and the owner read the reason (RN-15). |
| Personagem reservado | Reserved character | A character the master made for a player to take. It has no owner, and no player sees it, until one claims it (RN-10, MR-049). |
| Sessão de login | Sign-in session | A user's login in the app. Not the game session. |

## Game session

| Português | English | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aviso de sessão | Session notice | The in-app notice that a session started in one of the person's campaigns (RN-06). The app checks while the tab is visible; nothing arrives while the app is closed. |
| Evento da sessão | Session event | A change made at the table, kept in order and never altered. Together they are the session history (HP corrections, combat changes, undo). |
| Resumo da sessão | Session summary | What the table sees when the session ends: duration, combats, scenes and the combined highlights. The master sees every number; the player sees the winners and their own result (MR-032). |
| Sessão ao vivo | Live session | The open game session on the session page. Everyone sees changes at once, without reloading. |
| Sessão de jogo | Game session | One meeting of the table, numbered from 1 in each campaign. Only one is open at a time, and starting a session locks the sheets. |

## Characters and sheets

| Português | English | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Antecedente "Outro" | Custom background | The free-text background of the character editor, by the SRD 5.1 rule "Customizing a Background": a name, two skills, two tools or languages in total, one feature and the equipment. The sheet computes with it; what is missing is a warning, never an error. |
| Cópia de personagem | Character copy | A new character made from another, to play in another campaign. Once copied, each goes its own way. |
| Dica | Hint | Two meanings. On a sheet: a bonus or advantage that depends on the situation (Gnome Cunning), which the master decides when to apply. In a puzzle: a text the master releases, or that a player earns by passing a skill check against the master's DC (RN-27). |
| Documento da campanha | Campaign document | The master's private text for preparing the campaign: Markdown, with gallery images and links to maps and sheets (MR-018). Saving is refused if it changed after the app opened it. |
| Estado do personagem | Character state | Where the character is: draft, locked, dead or pending approval. |
| Estado (de uma característica) | Temporary state | A stance or form a table feature defines, turned on with "Ativar" and off with "Encerrar" (or by its duration, a rest or incapacity); other effects of the feature may hold only while it is on (MR-025). |
| Ficha | Sheet | A character's rules data. Full for player, enemy and boss; basic (HP, AC, speed, initiative and up to three attacks) for minion and story NPC. |
| História do personagem | Character story | Personality, appearance, backstory and allies. After the sheet lock, the player edits it only while the master allows. |
| Notas do mestre | Master notes | What the master writes about a character, per campaign. Never reaches the player (RN-11). |
| Pendência da ficha | Sheet issue | A choice outside the rules. The sheet opens all the same and shows the warning: the app helps, it does not judge. |
| Personagem | Character | Player, enemy, boss, minion or story NPC. A player character who dies is not deleted; it can be the base of another character or an NPC (RN-03, RN-04). |
| Revisão do personagem | Character revision | If the character changed after the app opened it, saving is refused and the person reloads. |
| Trava da ficha | Sheet lock | The moment the player stops editing the sheet: the start of the first session, or of the next session for a character created later. |

## Rules content and SRD terms

| Português | English | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Alvo da magia | Spell target | Whom a spell reaches: "Uma criatura", "Várias criaturas", an area (cone, cube, cylinder, line or sphere, in steps of 1.5 m: "Cone de 4,5 m") or "Só quem conjura". In combat, an area spell lets the caster choose the creatures; the app does not draw the area (RN-23). |
| Alcance Pessoal e Toque | Self and Touch range | "Pessoal" reaches only the caster or an area coming out of them; "Toque" is 1.5 m in combat. |
| Habilidade | Ability | One of Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma. It covers the score ("valor de habilidade"), the modifier and the check. The increase is the feature "Incremento no Valor de Habilidade". |
| Conteúdo de regras | Rules content (SRD 5.1) | The races, classes, spells and rules the app knows, from the SRD 5.1 (CC-BY-4.0). The screen does not show its version. |
| Conteúdo da mesa | Table content (homebrew) | Classes, subclasses, races, subraces, backgrounds, spells, feats, options, class features, creatures and rules outside the SRD that the master registers for one campaign (MR-025, MR-026, RN-23). Members read, only the master writes, and players can propose. Nothing is deleted; entries are archived. |
| Arquivar | Archive | Takes a table-content entry out of new choices without deleting it. Sheets that use it keep it. The master can unarchive ("Desarquivar"). |
| A classe mudou | "The class changed" | The sheet warning that a table-content entry it uses changed and the sheet no longer fits it, with the sentences of what no longer fits. It blocks no edit and goes away only when the sheet is fixed. |
| Menu de efeitos | Effect menu | The closed list of what a table feature can do (modifier, proficiency, resource, sense, advantage, action, extra attack, ranged attack, trigger on an event, choice, note, granted spell). It never runs code (RN-23). |
| Padrões da tabela de 20 níveis | 20-level table defaults | The numbers the class editor starts from, taken from the SRD class tables: proficiency bonus, ability score increase levels and a 20-row table per way of casting. |
| Conjurador de um terço | One-third caster | A table-content subclass that casts with a third of the class level (rounded down) while the parent class does not. The SRD 5.1 has none. |
| Talento | Feat | An optional rule: in place of an ability score increase, a character takes a feat. The SRD 5.1 has one (Agarrador); the master registers others. Offered at level-up only when the table rule "Talentos" is "permitidos" (RN-24, MR-025). |
| Pacote de conteúdo | Content pack | The table content of a campaign as one file: exported with "Exportar", imported with "Importar pacote" after a preview, all or nothing (MR-025). |
| Opções para os jogadores | Options for the players | The master's switches per class, subclass, race, background and spell. What is off never appears to players, and a parent that is off hides its children (RN-23). |
| Magias (consulta) | Spells (reference) | The page where players read every spell switched on for the table, with search and filters (MR-045). |
| Magia ativa | Active spell | A spell that took effect and lasts. It ends by "Encerrar", by concentration, or by a rest that covers its duration. Listed in "Magias ativas" (RN-32). |
| Ritual | Ritual | A spell with the ritual tag cast without a slot, taking its casting time plus 10 minutes. Only classes with Ritual Casting can; the screen offers "Como ritual" (RN-32). |
| Conjuração longa | Long casting | A cast that takes minutes or hours, outside combat. The caster concentrates, then the master taps "Concluir conjuração" when the time has passed, and only then is the slot spent. Broken concentration fails it with no slot spent (RN-32). |
| Magia que lê PV | HP-reading spell | A spell that depends on hit points, not damage: Sono and Leque Cromático (a dice total goes through creatures from lowest HP), Palavra de Poder Atordoar and Matar (an HP threshold), Estabilizar (only at 0 HP) and Cura Completa. The server uses the real HP; the player never sees an enemy's HP (RN-20). |
| Revivificar | Revivify | The SRD 5.1 spell that brings back a creature that died in the last minute, with 1 HP. In combat a minute is 10 rounds; out of combat the master confirms the time. |
| Sintonização | Attunement | A character's bond with a magic item that requires it; some items limit who can ("por um paladino"). |
| Raridade | Rarity | Common, uncommon, rare, very rare, legendary or artifact. "Varia" marks a family whose variants differ. |
| Família de itens, variante | Item family, variant | A magic item the SRD lists in several versions (Armor +1, +2, +3). In a treasure draw a family counts as one unit per rarity. |
| Nível de desafio (ND) | Challenge rating (CR) | A creature's strength, from 0 to 30. The SRD table gives its XP when defeated (CR 1/4 = 50 XP); the master can type another value. |
| Tamanho | Size | A creature's size, Tiny to Gargantuan. It decides who can pass through an enemy (two sizes of difference) and who shares a square (only Tiny with Tiny). Every combatant occupies one square. |

## Resources, rests and class features

| Português | English | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cura pelas Mãos, Conjuração Flexível, Metamagia, Inspiração de Bardo | Lay on Hands, Flexible Casting, Metamagic, Bardic Inspiration | Class resources with a flow of their own in combat: a pool healed by touch; sorcery points turned into slots and back; spell options paid in sorcery points; a die given to another creature and used after the d20 and before the result (RN-02). |
| Dados de vida por tipo | Hit dice by die size | The hit dice spent, kept per die size because a multiclass character has several sizes. A short rest spends one at a time and heals its roll plus the Constitution modifier. |
| Descanso curto, descanso longo | Short rest, long rest | The master's rests for the table (SRD 5.1). A short rest returns short-rest resources and pact slots; a long rest returns all spell slots and HP, ends temporary HP and returns half the hit dice (at least one). Refused while a combat runs (RN-02). |
| Forma Selvagem | Wild Shape | The druid's power to take the form of an SRD beast. The beast's sheet applies (AC, separate HP, speeds, attacks) while the druid keeps mental abilities and proficiencies. When the beast drops to 0 HP the druid returns and the leftover damage passes to their HP. |
| Frenesi | Frenzy | The Berserker's choice, when raging, to make a melee weapon attack as a bonus action each later turn and gain a level of exhaustion when the rage ends. |
| Gatilho num evento | Trigger on an event | A table feature's effect that runs on its owner's event: after a short or long rest, when a death save fails, when the owner drops to 0 hit points. It gives temporary hit points, heals, restores uses of a resource and may spend one to count at all. Only the owner's triggers run (MR-025). |
| Indomitável | Indomitable | The fighter's feature (levels 9, 13, 17): a failed saving throw may be rolled again and the new roll stands. The app asks "Falhou na salvaguarda. Usar Indomitável para rolar de novo?" after the result, behind Lucky and the Bardic Inspiration question (RN-35). |
| Golpe de Sorte | Stroke of Luck | The rogue's feature (level 20): a missed attack hits instead, or a failed ability check counts the d20 as 20, once per short or long rest. The app asks "Errou o ataque. Usar Golpe de Sorte para acertar?" or "Falhou no teste. Usar Golpe de Sorte para contar o d20 como 20?" after the result, behind Lucky and the Bardic Inspiration question (RN-35). |
| Pergunta pendente | Pending question | What a roll that waits asks the one who rolled before the result is told: first the halfling's "Tirou 1: rolar de novo?", then the Bardic Inspiration die. The d20 was rolled and kept and nothing is resolved or written. The roller's player answers it, and so does the master, who reads every pending question of the table; the first answer wins (RN-35, MR-014). |
| PV, espaços e dados de vida | HP, slots and hit dice | The numbers that change during play and last between sessions: current and temporary HP (lost first), spell and pact slots used, hit dice used. The maximums come from the sheet; the master corrects the values (RN-02). |
| Recuperação Arcana | Arcane Recovery | The wizard's once-per-long-rest feature: after a short rest, spell slots return up to half the wizard level in combined spell level, none of 6th or higher. |
| Recuperação Natural | Natural Recovery | The Circle of the Land druid's once-per-long-rest feature: after a short rest, spell slots return up to half the druid level in combined spell level, none of 6th or higher. |
| Recurso | Resource | An ability with limited uses (Retomar o Fôlego, Chi, Fúria). The sheet states the maximum and when it returns: on a short or long rest, at dawn, never, or (a table's) when its owner rolls initiative or by one use on a rest. A table action may cost several uses, or let the player choose how many to spend (MR-025). |
| Sortudo | Lucky | The halfling's trait: a 1 on an attack roll, ability check or saving throw may be rolled again and the new roll stands. The app asks "Tirou 1: rolar de novo?" before the result, ahead of the Bardic Inspiration question (RN-18, RN-35). |
| Ver pelos olhos do familiar | See through the familiar's eyes | A character with a familiar sees what it sees, with its senses, while it is on the same map within 30 m. The character is blinded and deafened meanwhile. |
| Subir de nível pela ficha | Level up from the sheet | Guided editing of the locked sheet while the character "Pode subir de nível": the player adds a class level and only their own choices; the rules engine checks it (RN-01, RN-12, MR-040). |
| Multiclasse | Multiclass | A character with more than one class. A new class needs the main ability of every class at 13 or more (SRD 5.1, "Multiclassing"; RN-12). |
| Nível de conjurador | Caster level | The sum that gives a multiclass character's slots: full levels of full casters, half of Paladin and Ranger, a third of casting Fighter or Rogue subclasses. Pact Magic does not count. |
| Escolha pendente | Pending choice | A pick a class or race asks for that the sheet lacks (Fighting Style, an invocation...). A new character cannot be created with one; a locked sheet completes it with "Completar escolhas pendentes" (RN-33). |
| O que mudou | What changed | The master's list of what the player chose at a level-up. The master does not approve or veto; they read it and can fix the sheet (MR-040). |
| Pode subir de nível | Can level up | The warning that a character already can: the XP reached the next level, or the master recorded a milestone (RN-12). |

## XP and treasure

| Português | English | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Marco | Milestone | In a milestone campaign, the master raises the party's level when the story reaches an agreed point. On "Registrar marco", the characters they pick are marked "Pode subir de nível" (RN-12). |
| Marco planejado | Planned milestone | A moment the master writes beforehand (up to 120 characters, at most 100 per campaign). Only the master sees the list; players see the reached ones (RN-09, MR-016). |
| Modo de XP | XP mode | How the campaign awards XP: by defeated enemies, by gold or by milestones. |
| Prêmio de XP | XP award | Each "Dar XP" or recorded milestone, with who gave it, why and each character's share. Only the last can be undone; an undone award stays, labelled "Desfeito". |
| Tesouro | Treasure | A map point with a value in PO, placed before the session. Once found, everyone who sees the map sees it (MR-041). |
| Voltar à cidade | Return to town | Converts the treasures the group found into XP in one gold-mode award: 1 XP per PO, split equally and rounded down among the characters the master picks. Each treasure is converted once (MR-041, RN-09). |
| Tesouro individual e de covil | Individual and hoard treasure | The two modes of the treasure generator: what a creature carries (coins only), and what a lair keeps (coins, gems, art objects, magic items). The same mode, party level and seed always give the same treasure (MR-044). |
| PC, PP, PE, PO, PL | CP, SP, EP, GP, PP | Copper, silver ("peça de prata"; 10 PP = 1 PO), electrum (2 PE = 1 PO), gold and platinum (1 PL = 10 PO). 100 PC = 1 PO. Magic items never become XP (MR-044, RN-09). |
| Valores do SRD 5.2.1 (regras de 2024) | SRD 5.2.1 values | The label on magic item prices, which come from a SRD 5.2.1 table. A consumable is worth half; the Spell Scroll is worth the full value. |
| Orçamento de XP | XP budget | The XP the party can spend on an encounter at low, moderate and high difficulty, from the SRD 5.2.1 table. The party is the living player characters plus the NPCs the master adds (MR-043). |
| Dificuldade do encontro | Encounter difficulty | The lowest band whose budget fits the encounter's total XP: "Baixa", "Moderada", "Alta" or "Acima de alta" (allowed, with a warning). No multipliers. The strongest creature can have a CR up to the party's lowest level plus 3. |
| Encontro guardado | Saved encounter | An encounter the master saves on a battle point; "Começar este combate" puts its monsters in combat. Only the master sees it (RN-10). |
| Bestiário | Bestiary | The 334 SRD creatures with their sheets, where the master makes an NPC ("Criar NPC") or puts monsters in combat ("Pôr no combate"). Players never see it (MR-042, RN-29). |

## Scenes, notes and the table

| Português | English | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Anotação | Note | A player's private text (up to 2,000 characters, 300 per campaign), optionally labelled with a discovered scene. Not even the master reads it (MR-030). |
| Ações da cena | Scene action | A check the master puts on a scene: a skill, ability check or saving throw, with an optional name and DC. The player sees their own bonus; the DC only if "Mostrar a CD aos jogadores" is on. |
| Cena de RP | RP scene | A moment outside combat: a point of interest of the scene kind, opened in the session for everyone to see. |
| Cena descoberta | Discovered scene | An RP scene the group has already seen, or that a player saw alone while it was open to only some players. Only a scene discovered by the player can label their note. |
| Conversa da cena | The scene's conversation | The session's live text feed (MR-051): the master narrates and speaks as NPCs, players speak as their character or out of character. Each line is for the whole table or for chosen readers (RN-36). |
| Em cena (o palco) | On stage | The NPCs the master puts on the open scene, up to 4 at a time, one speaking. The player sees only the name and portrait (MR-031). |
| Escondida dos jogadores | Hidden from the players | What the master keeps from the players on a scene: an NPC on the stage ("Escondida dos jogadores", against "Os jogadores veem") or an action ("Escondida"). No player receives it, in an answer, a live hint or an image; the master reads it marked (MR-031, MR-015, RN-10). |
| Falar como | Speak as | Who a line is from: the master chooses the narrator or an NPC of the campaign; a player chooses their own character or out of character. |
| Fora do personagem | Out of character | A player's line that is not the character speaking; it shows the player's display name. |
| Ganchos, anotações e pistas | Hooks, notes and clues | The master's private text on an RP scene. A clue is a short sentence the master reveals to chosen players, who keep it as "Pista do mestre" (MR-029). |
| Jogador removido | Removed player | What the feed shows as the author of a line when the author's account was erased. The line stays. |
| Narrador | Narrator | The master's voice when no NPC is speaking. It has one voice of its own when a line is heard. |
| Ouvir | Hear | The button that plays a narrator or NPC line with a generated voice. The audio is made the first time a reader asks and kept with the line (RN-36). |
| Entrar na cena | Join the scene | What a player does in a scene open to only some players when the master allows it ("Os jogadores podem entrar"): they read its name, tap the button and become one of its players; the master sees that they joined by themselves (MR-015, RN-10). |
| Quem está na cena | Who is in the scene | The players a scene is open to: the whole table (a later joiner included) or the ones the master picks and may change while it is open. A player who is out gets no scene at all, as when none is open; the master reads who is in and who is out (MR-015, RN-10). |
| Quem vê a imagem | Who sees the image | The audience of the image the master shows: the whole table ("Mostrar a toda a mesa"), the scene's players or chosen players ("Mudar quem vê"). A player outside it never receives the image (MR-028, RN-10). |
| Retrato do NPC | NPC portrait | A gallery image on an NPC's sheet, shown on its card (master only) and in the scene while it is on stage. |
| Rolagem da cena | Scene roll | The d20 of a scene action plus the bonus (RN-18). The player sees only their own totals, and "Passou" or "Não passou" only if the master shows the DC. Rolls stop at the attempt limit ("Sem mais tentativas"). |
| Rolagem livre | Free roll | A roll of any dice (d4 to d100, 1 to 20 of them, a modifier from -20 to +20) that a member makes with "Dados" (MR-052), outside the game's rules. The app always rolls it, whatever the table's dice mode (RN-18). A player's goes to the whole table; the master chooses "Para a mesa" or "Só eu", and a "Só eu" roll reaches no player (RN-10). |
| Tentativas | Attempts | How many times each player can roll a scene action while it is open (1 by default, 1 to 5, or unlimited). Reopening the scene resets the counts (MR-015). |
| Mostrar a CD aos jogadores | Show the DC to players | The per-scene switch that decides whether players see DCs and whether their rolls passed. Off by default; the master always sees it (MR-015, RN-20). |
| Teste em grupo | Group check | One check that two or more characters make; the group passes when at least half pass (RN-34). |
| Regras da mesa | Table rules | The page (`/campaigns/:id/rules`) with the master's choices (HP on level-up, ways of making ability scores, the critical hit, who sees death saves, XP mode, dice, grid) and "Lembretes da mesa" (RN-24). |
| Estilo da mesa | Table style | "Tudo no app", "Mesa física", "Teatro da mente" or "Personalizado". A style only fills in defaults, which stay editable (RN-24). |
| Lembrete da mesa | House rule reminder | A short note the master writes for the table (up to 20, 200 characters each). The app shows it and never enforces it (RN-24). |
| 4d6 guardado | Stored 4d6 | Six 4d6 rolls (drop the lowest die) made once for a player's next sheet and kept until the sheet uses them (RN-24). |
| Dado físico ou do app | Physical or app dice | How the player rolls: in the app, or with real dice, typing the result. The master decides whether the campaign allows choosing (RN-18). |
| Pacote da campanha | Campaign package | The `.meurpg.zip` file with what the master prepared (document, gallery, maps, NPCs, scenes, puzzles, table rules and content, characters as reserved) and never players' data, sessions or accounts. Exporting keeps the file 24 hours; importing creates a new campaign, all or nothing. |

## Gallery and maps

| Português | English | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Galeria | Gallery | A campaign's images, uploaded by the master (MR-019). A player sees an image only while it is shown to them, on a map they see, or left with them. |
| Imagem gerada | Generated image | A gallery image made by an AI model (MR-039, RN-28): scene art, the isometric view of a map, or the textured map. Those made from a map show only what players see now. It starts hidden and carries the provider's invisible watermark. |
| Vaga do mês | Monthly slot | One of the images a campaign can generate per month (RN-28). Returned if nothing is generated; spent if the image comes back. |
| Imagem mostrada, Imagem deixada | Shown image, left image | The master shows one image at a time to the players (MR-028). "Deixar com os jogadores" keeps it on their page under "Imagens que o mestre deixou" until "Tirar". |
| Mapa | Map | A gallery image with points of interest and tokens. Born hidden; a player sees it after the master reveals it or while it is the current map (MR-008). |
| Mapa atual | Current map | The map the master shows everyone in the open session. A new session starts with none. |
| Ponto de interesse | Point of interest | A place on the map that opens a battle, a submap or an RP scene. Born hidden. |
| Submapa | Submap | A map opened from a point of interest of another map. |
| Token | Token | The marker of a character on a map. A player's is born visible, an NPC's hidden. |
| Revelar, escondido | Reveal, hidden | Showing something to the players or hiding it again (RN-10). What is hidden never leaves the server for the player. On the master's screen it appears dashed, with "Escondido". |
| Calibração da grade | Grid calibration | How much each square of a map's drawing is worth ("cada quadrado deste desenho vale 3 m"). The app keeps counting in squares of 1.5 m (RN-25). |
| Grade | Grid | The square grid of the map in combat. Each square is 1.5 m, diagonals included (RN-21). |
| Folha de impressão | Print sheet | A piece of the map printed on one page at the table's scale, named by row letter and column number (B2) (MR-033). |
| Masmorra, gerador de masmorras | Dungeon, dungeon generator | A map with walls, floor, doors, traps and chests. The generator makes one from a size, options and a seed as a map the master can edit; "Redesenhar" redraws the image from the current walls and doors (MR-010). |
| Camada do mapa | Map layer | One of the things the master paints on the squares: difficult terrain, wall, cover, light and doors. |
| Pintar | Paint | The editor mode in which the master paints layers with a brush (1×1 or 3×3). "Tudo salvo" says it arrived. Desktop only (MR-034). |
| Porta | Door | A map layer: open, closed, locked, grate or secret. A closed one blocks sight, light and cover; a secret one is a wall to the players until revealed (RN-26). |
| Parede | Wall | A square that blocks movement, sight and light. It is total cover (RN-21, MR-036). |
| Cobertura | Cover | Half (+2 AC and Dexterity checks), three-quarters (+5) or total (cannot be targeted). Marked by the master and computed on every attack; degrees do not add up (MR-034). |
| Terreno difícil | Difficult terrain | A square where each step costs double. A creature's space also counts (MR-034). |
| Armadilha | Trap | A hidden point with the DCs to notice and to find, a trigger and an effect. A player sees only a trap their character noticed or found, or that the master revealed, and never the DCs or the effect (MR-035). |
| Percepção passiva | Passive Perception | 10 plus the Perception bonus, without rolling. It is what notices a trap in passing, with −5 in dim light (MR-035). |
| Luz, luz de base | Light, base light | A light source placed on the map (bright and dim radii in metres), and the map's default light (Bright, Dim or Dark) where no source reaches (MR-036). |
| Penumbra | Dim light | The weak light at a source's edge: the place is lightly obscured and passive Perception there loses 5. |
| Visão no escuro | Darkvision | Seeing in the dark within a range: dim light counts as bright and darkness as dim, in greys (MR-036). |
| Névoa de guerra | Fog of war | The map shows each player only what their character sees; what was seen is dimmed. The player never receives the original image of a fog map. The master sees everything (MR-036). |
| Visão do grupo | Group vision | A per-map option: every player sees the union of what all player characters see. Off by default. |
| Ver como | View as | The master opens a map as a player character and sees exactly what that player receives (MR-036). |
| Esquecer o que foi visto | Forget what was seen | The master erases the players' memory of a fog map (MR-036). |

## Combat

| Português | English | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Economia de ação | Action economy | What a character spends in a turn: one action, one bonus action, one reaction and movement (MR-014). |
| Aba do combate | Combat tab | When a player plays several combatants, the turn bar has one tab per character and per group of creatures (MR-037). |
| Ataque de oportunidade | Opportunity attack | A melee attack off turn that spends the reaction. Leaving an enemy's reach provokes it; the server offers it to whoever controls the enemy (RN-21). |
| Ataque Extra | Extra Attack | The feature that lets the Attack action make more than one attack. |
| Combatente | Combatant | A character inside an encounter. An NPC enters as copies ("Goblin 1", "Goblin 2"). |
| Concentração | Concentration | Maintaining a spell that requires it. Casting another ends the first; when the character takes damage the app reminds the check (DC 10 or half the damage). The app reminds, the table rolls (RN-22, RN-32). |
| Condição | Condition | An SRD label the master sets on a combatant: Agarrado, Amedrontado, Atordoado, Cego, Derrubado, Enfeitiçado, Envenenado, Exaustão, Impedido, Incapacitado, Inconsciente, Invisível, Paralisado, Petrificado, Surdo. The app shows and reminds and applies no effect, except where a spell grants it (Sono gives Inconsciente) (RN-22). |
| Agarrar, empurrar, escapar | Grapple, shove, escape | Special melee actions decided by a contest (RN-34). |
| Criatura da mesa | Table creature | An SRD creature with the master's own hit points, armor class, damage bonus and text traits, registered as table content (RN-23). The creature list and sheet (master and holder), giving it to a character, combat and "Criar NPC" read it by key. Summoning spells, Wild Shape and the encounter tools offer SRD creatures only. A player reads it only while one of their characters holds it. |
| Criatura do personagem | Character creature | A creature a player controls: Forma Selvagem, a familiar, the undead of Animar Mortos, the animals of Conjurar Animais. It has its own sheet and acts in combat (MR-037). |
| Grupo de criaturas | Creature group | The creatures of one casting. They roll one initiative and play a joint turn (MR-037). |
| Familiar | Familiar | The spirit Convocar Familiar calls: one at a time, moves and acts but does not attack (Pacto da Corrente allows an attack with the reaction). A 1-hour ritual that costs no slot. |
| Convocação | Summoning | A spell that creates creatures for the character. |
| Dano pendente | Pending damage | The damage of a hit between the d20 and the end. The master applies ("Aplicar 5 de dano") or discards ("Não aplicar") (RN-02). |
| Desfazer | Undo | Master only. Steps back the last action; the history keeps both (RN-02). |
| Desengajar | Disengage | The action that stops the turn's movement from provoking opportunity attacks. |
| Disputa | Contest | Two creatures roll against each other; the higher total wins and a tie changes nothing (RN-34). |
| Encontro | Encounter | A combat with initiative, rounds and turns. A session has at most one that has not ended. |
| Esconder-se | Hide | A Dexterity (Stealth) check against passive Perception. A hidden attacker has advantage (RN-34). |
| Estado do inimigo | Enemy state | The word a player sees instead of an NPC's HP: Ileso, Ferido, Muito ferido or Derrotado (RN-20). |
| Caído, estável, morrendo | Down, stable, dying | A character at 0 HP making death saves; at 0 HP with three successes; at 0 HP with three failures awaiting the master's confirmation. Only the master sees "Morrendo"; players see "Caído" (RN-03). Not the condition "Derrubado". |
| Iniciativa | Initiative | The roll that sets turn order. Each combatant rolls its own, even identical NPCs. A tie is decided by the master (RN-19). |
| Levantar-se | Stand up | The action of a prone combatant: costs half the turn's speed (RN-34). |
| Derrubado | Prone | The SRD condition of lying on the ground. Not "Caído". |
| Ajudar | Help | Gives an ally advantage on the next check or attack until used or the helper's next turn (RN-34). |
| Letargia | Lethargy | The turn in which a creature that ended Velocidade can neither move nor act. |
| Exaustão | Exhaustion | The six levels of the SRD condition; a long rest lowers one (RN-22). |
| Efeito | Lasting effect | Something a spell or the master puts on a creature for a while: a condition with an end, a bonus, an extra action. Counted in rounds in combat and in game time outside (RN-22). |
| Tempo de jogo | Game time | Time in the fiction; a round is 6 seconds. It moves outside combat only when the master moves it. |
| Janela de reação | Reaction window | The question "you may react?" that holds the triggering action until answered. A player reads only their own; for an NPC's, "Esperando o mestre". |
| Reação | Reaction | An action once per round, returning at the start of the combatant's turn: Escudo Arcano, Esquiva Sobrenatural, Defletir Projéteis, Repreensão Infernal, Contramágica, Palavras de Interrupção, Queda Suave and the opportunity attack. |
| Registro do combate | Combat log | What happened, last round first. A player gets only what they see; the master gets everything, with "Só o mestre vê" (RN-20). |
| Destaques do combate | Combat highlights | The end-of-combat screen: who dealt the most damage, healed the most, took the most, landed the most killing blows and critical hits. Ties name everyone (MR-032). |
| Surpresa | Surprise | A creature that cannot move, act or react until its first turn ends (RN-34). |
| Teste contra a morte | Death saving throw | The roll of a character at 0 HP: 10 or more succeeds, a natural 1 counts two failures, a natural 20 returns them with 1 HP. On the third failure they die only when the master confirms (RN-03). |
| Reviver | Revive | The master's power to bring a dead player character back with 1 HP, refused while the player has another living character (RN-03). Not a spell. |
| Turno conjunto | Joint turn | Combatants with the same initiative total who play a single turn, each with their own economy (MR-013, RN-19). A player sees only the group that has a player character (RN-20). |
| Vez do mestre | Master's turn | What a player sees when the combatant acting is hidden from them (RN-10, RN-20). |
| Lado | Side | Party or enemies. It decides who can pass through whom and who provokes. The master marks an NPC "Aliado" to put it on the party's side (MR-034). |
| Salto | Jump | Distance by Strength with a run-up of at least 3 m; it costs its length in movement (MR-034). |
| Movimento forçado | Forced movement | A move the master makes for a teleport, push or pull; it provokes nothing. The box "Movimento forçado" turns it on for one drag. |
| Teatro da mente | Theatre of the mind | Combat without a map or grid, chosen at the start: the master judges range, movement is a number ("Gastar movimento"), and the opportunity attack is offered by the master ("Oferecer ataque de oportunidade"). No fog, traps or summoned-creature position (RN-25). |

## Puzzles

| Português | English | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quebra-cabeça | Puzzle | A challenge the master creates and the players solve live in a scene: "Apagar as luzes", combination lock, spinning symbols, riddle, sequence or cipher (MR-038, RN-27). The server checks the solution and the player never receives it. |
| Ao errar | On a wrong move | What a wrong move triggers, chosen by the master and combinable: a trap, a spent attempt, a limit of moves or time that stops the puzzle (RN-27). |
| Ao resolver | On solve | What happens when it is solved: notify (default), open a door, reveal a map point or reveal a clue (RN-27). |
| Chave da cifra | Cipher key | The shift or keyword of a cipher. It is a scene clue the players find; the server never sends it with the puzzle. |
| Enigma, sequência e cifra | Riddle, sequence and cipher | Three puzzle types: a typed answer; repeat what the master played; decode a message with a key found as a clue (RN-27). |
| Informação dividida | Split information | Each player sees only the part of the clue the master gave them (up to 8 parts); others only know who has a part (RN-27). |
| Mural | Mural | The target of the spinning symbols. Players read it; the challenge is the moves (RN-10, RN-27). |
| Parar | Stop | A puzzle that reached its limit unsolved. Nobody plays until the master restarts or closes it. |
| Recomeçar, Gerar outro começo | Restart, Generate another start | "Recomeçar" returns to the same start; "Gerar outro começo" (lights and symbols only) draws a new, never-solved one (RN-27). |
| Runa, símbolo | Rune, symbol | A face of a lock wheel (Lua, Chama, Onda, Raiz, Olho, Estrela, Pedra, Folha) or a pillar (Corvo, Lobo, Serpente, Coruja, Cervo, Peixe). The name is always written beside the drawing. |
| Tentativa, rodada | Attempt, round | A wrong move a player may make in a round (1 to 10 per player); "Recomeçar" gives them back (RN-27). |
| Tocar a sequência | Play the sequence | The master shows the bells one step at a time; players repeat it afterwards (MR-038). |
