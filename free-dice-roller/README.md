# Free dice roller (B5): harness captures

**These are HARNESS CAPTURES, not the live stack** (no Docker daemon or CockroachDB was available). The real components (`app-free-dice-sheet` opened through `openFreeDice` as a bottom sheet / dialog, `app-free-roll-card`, `app-free-roll-log`, `app-session-header`, `app-roll-overlay` fed by `freeRollShow`, and the real `FreeDiceState`) were rendered in Chromium with fake data: a fake `list`/`roll` client answers the rolls, nothing ran against the server, the stream or the database. The surrounding page (top bar, "Mapa" placeholder) is a stand-in. Rendered from commit `01d5e6183` (branch `feat/free-dice-roller`) by a throwaway harness in a detached worktree, never committed to the feature branch. Fonts are the app's self-hosted ones.

Matrix: widths 390 (844 and also 667 high for the sheet states) (bottom sheet) and 1280 (440 px dialog), light and dark (`prefers-color-scheme`), device scale 1. File name: `dados-<state>-<role>-<width>-<theme>.png` (role `jogadora` = player, `mestre` = master). Viewport shots (the table overlay and the header are viewport / element shots).

States: `formulario` (player and master, 2d6 + 3), `formulario-segredo` (master, 1d20 + Vantagem + "Só eu"), `resultado` (2d6 + 3 = 11 (4, 4); player and master), `vantagem` ("descartado"), `natural-20`, `natural-1`, `vinte-dados` (wrapping), `d100`, `resultado-segredo` (master, "Só você"), `dados-fisicos` (note + "Rolar no app"), `lista-vazia`, `lista-carregando`, `lista-falhou`, `rolando` ("Rolando…" + "?" dice), `recusada` (rate limit, "em 3 segundos"), `sem-conexao` (button off + "Reconectando… Não dá para rolar sem conexão."), `cabecalho-normal|rolando|sem-conexao` (390), `registro` (1280, "Registro da sessão"; the master's list has a "Só você" line, the player's does not), `mesa-brisa-rolou` (table overlay, "Rolagem livre · 2d6 + 3").

## Defects against the board

1. **Fixed (was High), 390 x 844 and 390 x 667:** the primary button was below the fold in the master's form and in every state with a footer notice. The sheet now opens with its own panel class (`mr-sheet-dice`, `styles.scss`): the container is `calc(100dvh - 40px)` and the frame `calc(100dvh - 56px)`, so the container never scrolls and the body scrolls inside the sheet while the header and the footer stay. Measured in Chromium for `formulario` (both roles), `formulario-segredo` (master, chooser and advantage open, the longest form), `dados-fisicos`, `sem-conexao`, `recusada`, `resultado`, `resultado-segredo` and the others, light and dark: the "Rolar ..." button is 48 px high and its bottom is at y 828 of 844 (603 to 651 of 667 on the short phone), always inside the viewport, and the container does not scroll. The 667 px set is `dados-<state>-<role>-390x667-<theme>.png`.
2. **Fixed (was Low):** the "de novo" label no longer wraps: "Mudar dados" keeps its width and the primary takes the rest of the row or a row of its own (`free-dice-sheet.scss`), measured 48 px high (one line) for "Rolar 1d20 + 5 com vantagem de novo".
3. **Low, overlay:** the total in "Brisa rolou" is a small 20 px sans line (`shared/roll-overlay/roll-overlay.scss:242-246`) where the board shows a large display-face "11" under the dice (B5-06 state 12). Fix: a larger display-face size for `.result__line` when the roll is a free roll (or a `result--big` modifier).
4. **Low, "Registro da sessão":** the board splits the log into "Jogadoras e mesa" and "O mestre lê também" and adds the line "A rolagem secreta não existe para os jogadores..."; the component renders one newest-first list with the "Só você" badge (`pages/live-session/free-dice/free-roll-log.ts`, template). The badge keeps the meaning, so this is a visual deviation only.
5. **Low, copy:** the rolling card says "Você rola 1d20…" where the board says "Brisa rola 2d6 + 3…" (name + formula) (`free-dice-sheet.html`, `card--rolling`).
