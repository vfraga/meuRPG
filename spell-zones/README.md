# Spell zones (B6): harness captures

**These are HARNESS CAPTURES, not the live stack** (no Docker daemon or CockroachDB was available). The real components were rendered in Chromium with fake data: `app-combat-map-card` (with `app-zone-layer`, the zone legend), `app-zones-panel`, `ZoneMoveSheet` + `app-zone-move-map` and `EffectSaveSheet` (both opened through the real `openSheet`: bottom sheet at 390, dialog from 768), `app-reaction-queue`, `app-log-list` (lines built by `logGroups`), `app-pending-damages`, with the view-models of `core/combat/zones.ts` and the fixtures `spellZone()` / `combatant()` / `encounter()` of `core/combat/combat-testing.ts`. A fake `CombatClient` answers nothing; nothing ran against the server, the stream or the database. Rendered from commit `33feab869` (branch `feat/spell-zones-web`) by a throwaway harness in a detached worktree, never committed to the feature branch. The `zonas-mover-*`, `zonas-mover-longe-*`, `zonas-aviso-teste-*`, `zonas-aviso-teia-*` and `zonas-aviso-teste-cd-conjurador-*` files at 390 were re-rendered after the fixes below, from commit `b7c3a52d1` (same harness; `medidas-correcoes.json` holds the measurements); the move sheet also has `-390x667-` files (390 x 667 viewport). Everything else is still from `33feab869`. Fonts are the app's self-hosted ones. The surrounding page (top bar, "Rodada 3 / Vez de ..." card, the cave picture, its walls and terrain) is a stand-in drawn by the harness, so the background and the map art differ from the boards.

Matrix: widths 390, 768 and 1280, light and dark (`prefers-color-scheme`), device scale 1 (390 x 844, 768 x 1024, 1280 x 900). File name: `zonas-<state>-<role>-<width>-<theme>.png` (roles `mestre`, `jogador`, `conjurador` = the caster's own player; themes `claro`, `escuro`). Pages are full-page shots; sheets are viewport shots. `medidas-e-auditoria.json` holds the measurements and the layout audit of every file.

## States

- `mapa` (mestre, jogador): the map card with the zone layer, labels and legend, and "Zonas ativas". Master: four zones (Raio Lunar, Nuvem de Adagas, Teia, and Nuvem Mortal of a hidden caster, dashed, "Só você vê") with rounds left, concentration, "Aqui: ...", save, damage, notes. Player: three zones of other casters (name and area only) and Espíritos Guardiões of the player's own character with the details.
- `encerrar` (mestre): the end confirm in place under the first card (the whole page) and `...-cartao.png` (the card alone).
- `aviso-teste` (jogador): the target player's save prompt, **no "CD"**. `aviso-teste-cd` (conjurador): the same prompt for the caster's player, with "CD 14" (the master's queue card is in `estados`). `aviso-teia` (jogador): Teia, "contido ... Soltar-se gasta uma ação". `aviso-dado-fisico` (jogador): "Digitar o resultado" opened.
- `mover` (mestre, jogador): "Mover o Raio Lunar", the picked square 4,5 m away, current footprint dimmed, preview, "Mover a zona". `mover-longe` (jogador): a square past 18 m, "Longe demais: a área anda até 18 m do ponto atual. Faltam 1,5 m." (the harness picks a void square to get beyond the range on a 20-column map).
- `estados` (mestre column, one page): empty list (nothing drawn; dashed box is the harness's), log lines (zone moved, concentration replaced, duration, concentration ended, ended by master, started the turn in a zone and failed, entered and took damage), the generic pending damage "Zona de magia: Nuvem de Adagas", and the master's reaction-queue card for a Goblin's zone save (with "CD 14").

Até 18 m do ponto atual. Gasta a ação de Bartolo."), legend, summary and out-of-range notice; "Soltar-se gasta uma ação"; no horizontal page scroll anywhere.

## Layout audit (`auditLayout` of `e2e/tests/layout.ts`, as `expectAligned` runs it)

Run on every one of the 66 screens (the six `-cartao` crops are element shots of the same page) (icon alignment, text over an icon, tile padding, controls outside their `.mr-panel`): **0 findings**, on every screen and theme. (The audit was checked to be alive: it flags a deliberately off-centre icon.) CI's real-stack run of `a11y.spec.ts` should therefore find nothing new in these components.
